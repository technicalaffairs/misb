مقدمة

This document out line the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

Caustic dissolver tank transfer pump is provided for transferring the prepared caustic solution to caustic storage tank

Technical Specification for condensate polisher caustic dissolver pump  (Quantity: 8)

Type:                            Horizontal Centrifugal

Rating:                          75 LPM (20 GPM)

Pump head:                  9.1 M (30FT)

Design temp.:                20 C (68F)

Drive HP:                     2 HP

MFR:                           Alice Chalmers

No. of pumps:              8

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, ... etc

  • Isolate the circuit breaker for electrical equipments

  • Contact thermometer
