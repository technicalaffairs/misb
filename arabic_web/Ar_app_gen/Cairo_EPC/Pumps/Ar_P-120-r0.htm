مقدمة

This document outline the equipment inspection checklist I2,  which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

The transfer Pump function is to transfer Mazout from storage thank to day tanks and to recirculate the mazout to storage tank if day tank fuel to keep the system warm and ready 3 pumps per unit.

The technical specification of the condensate pump:  
R.P.M = 1450            Suction pressure: -0.63 Kg/cm2
T = 60 C               Discharge pressure: 4.64 Kg/cm2        

 HP = 93.2                    Q = 5128 LPM

Safety Precautions

  • Work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should the suitable safety category

  • Safety fence like ropes with marks surrounding the work area

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes, gloves etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker

  • Disconnect electric element terminals of motor

  • Closing V/V delivery

Tools and Equipment

  • Standard tool set

  • Dial indicator

  • Oil can, drain pan, grease gun

  • Oil DTE heavy

  • Grease Mobilplex 47

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Change gear box oil MOBIL.

  2. Relubricate P/P bearings

  3. Inspect shaft packing and keep packing leakage not more than 28 drops per mint and change the packing if needed (Packing = 1/2" graphite)

  4. Check P/P discharge press. and flow rate (in normal Pdisch= 4.6 Kg/cm2 Flow rate = 5128 L/min)

  5. Check P/P motor alignment by readjusting the straightness of the pump and the motor together.
