مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1 which must be performed on a monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the title block.

The burner fuel oil pumps (qty =3 /unit) have capacity of 8701 L/mint at 3.9Kg/Cm2 and 60C each during normal operation two P/Ps are in service and the other in stand by the discharge press. of the pumps is controlled by a control valve, which recirculates the oil back to the day tank the suction of the burner oil pumps are connected to a common header the suction header is connected to the day tank the burner fuel oil pump have a common discharge.

The discharge header of the pumps supply the pressure control line and burner oil heaters through the flow indicator and transmitting station.

احتياطات السلامة

  • Work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, shoes, earplugs etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the rotary fuel oil pumps.

  • Disconnect electric terminals of rotary fuel oil motor.

  • The pump P/P must be isolated mechanically by closing valve discharge and delivery.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Standard tool set, dial indicator, clamp feeler

  • الأسلاك والرفع المعدات

 

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check free movement of pump shaft or make balance for the shaft

  2. Check pump bearing temperature .

  3. Check the oil and change if needed or change the bearing.

  4. Check for abnormal noise and repair the cause of this noise.

  5. Check pump discharge press. and capacity.

  6. Inspect the stages of the pump and repair or change if needed.
