مقدمة

This document out line the equipment inspection checklist I3, which must be performed every two years as indicated by Y2  in the title block.

Each blow down sump is equipped with two blow down sump pumps, which is vertically mounted submerged pumps, these pumps discharge the condensate to waste treatment system.

Safety Precautions

  • Work order must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, shoes, gloves, earplugs etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of pump motor.

  • Disconnect electric terminals of motor.

Tools and Equipment

  • Standard tool set

  • Contact thermometer

  • Adjustable wrench

  • Temp. indicator

  • Grease : Mobilplex

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Tighten all loose bolts and check for excessive vibration & listen for abnormal noise, then change any of the defected internal parts.

  2. Check pump discharge press.

  3. Relubricate pump bearing and check bearing temperature (max 85 ◦C) and change the bearing if needed.

  4. Clean impeller from excessive erosion, corrosion or cracking by removing the layer of corrosion and repairing the cracks by welding and make balance.

  5. Check bearing cleaning housing to prevent any deposits

  6. Check alignment between motor shaft and pump, readjust again and operate the pump to check the work.
