مقدمة

This document outlines  the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed every six  months as indicated by  M6 in the title block.

This pump operate in the condensate polishing system, acid metering pump is required for regeneration of the cation resin and caustic metering pump is required for regeneration of the anion resin

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, ... etc

  • Isolate the circuit breaker for electrical equipments

Tools and Equipment

  • Standard tool set

  • Oil can

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check gear driven oil level monthly and add oil as required oil delvac 1240

  2. Change gear box oil delvac 1240

  3. Change hydraulic fluid ATF 220

  4. Check and record vibration
