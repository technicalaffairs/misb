مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2 which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

The burner fuel oil boosters pumps (3 per unit) have capacity of 908 L/mint at a discharge pressure of 83 kg/Cm2 and 115C each under normal operation two pumps are in service the discharge pressure of the pumps is controlled by pressure control valve that recirculates the oil back to the suction of the burner fuel oil heaters

Safety Precautions

  • Work order must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, shoes, gloves, earplugs etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the fan motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of fan motor

  • The fan must be isolated mechanically by closing delivery damper

Tools and Equipment

  • Contact thermometer

  • Standard set of tools

Work to be Carried Out

  1. Check bearing temperature so change the oil if needed or change the internal parts of bearing .

  2. Check for excessive vibration

  3. Check the stages and the other parts of the pump and change the defected parts.

  4. Listen for unusual noise so check the bearing and the motor so change the defected parts.

  5. Check mech. seal for leakage and change the mech. seal if needed.

  6. Prior to stopping pump check bearing temperature and measure vibration (normal 4.8 mm/s) change oil

  7. Check the P/P for capacity and discharge press. and repair or change the parts to restore the pressure discharge.
