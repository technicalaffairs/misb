مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, which must be performed every three months as indicated by M3 in the title block.

The condensate pump function is to supply dearator with condensate water from hot well of main condenser

The technical specification of the condensate pump:   (Qty = 4)

Pump size: 269 APKD 5 Stages
Driver: 1500 HP Siemens all is motor
P/P rating: 4500 GMP-1480 rpm
 N.P.S.H.A: 1.83 feet from mounting
Total head: 1070 feet
Suction pressure: 14.7 psig
Discharge pressure: 463 psig
Brake hours power: 1460
 Bearing lubrication: Liquid pumped
