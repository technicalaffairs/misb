مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

Horizontal 6.3 kv motor Ansaldo of Forced draft fan (bearing lubricating oil)

The first draft initiated by eng. Ahmed EL-husany, tested by eng. Tarek EL-kafafy, accepted by general director eng. Nady Mosaed, approved by eng. Mohamed EL-Tahawy Head sector of Shoubra EL-Kheima PS.

This document revised by eng. Nageh Soliman Shousha CEPC quality control director, and certified by eng. AfafEbrahim El Dib, CEPC inspection & quality control 's  head sector & MPIS coordinator.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Wear your safety helmet & safety shoes.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • After stopping to motor insure that circuit breaker of the motor is draw out .

  • Avoid contact with rotating parts .

  • Act with care in accordance with prescribed procedures , in handling and lifting this equipment .

Tools and Equipment

  • Electrical tool box .

  • Vibration monitor .

  • Megger 1000 V

  • Avometer .

  • Suitable lifting device .

  • Resistor Kelvin bridge .

العمل المراد تنفيذه

 

Check the running conditions while the motor in service :-

  1. Check the motor vibration not the exceed 60 microns .

  2. Check that there is no abnormal noise on the motor .

  3. Check the bearing temp. on the Accurex in the control room not to exceed 87 C .

  • Check the motor stator temp. on the Accurex in the control room not the exceed 150 C .

  • Check the motor current reading of the 3 phases in the control room .

  • Check for any oil leak .

    • After isolation the circuit breaker of the motor

    1. Disconnect all cables of supply , protection , and the accessories .

    2. Uncouple the motor and remove the coupling flange .

    3. Remove the protection devices and the accessories of the bearing .

    4. Disassemble the heat exchanger, which consists of a bank of straight tubes arranged in a box located on the top of motor frame by the lifting plugs.

    5. Clean the inside of the motor (the rotor and stator), ventilation ducts and the windings by dry compressed air .

    6. Measure insulation resistance with surge arrestors disconnected, the insulation should be 100 MΩ or greater after one min.

    7. Measure the winding DC resistance , must be the same for 3 phase windings .

    8. Remove the bearing cap & check the condition of the white metal and the clearances between shaft & holes , change the bearing if it exceeded 0.3 mm .

    9. Remove the cover and the baffle of the cooling fan casing and clean it .

    10. Reassemble the heat exchanger and check all bolts are well tightened .

    11. Change the bearing oil (Type DTE26)

    12. Connect all cables (Power supply , protection and accessories) .

    13. Tighten the ground wire.

    14. Run the motor on  no-load for enough time and check the running conditions .

    15. Stop the motor and couple the motor with the fan shaft then run the motor & increase the load slowly with checking the running conditions.
