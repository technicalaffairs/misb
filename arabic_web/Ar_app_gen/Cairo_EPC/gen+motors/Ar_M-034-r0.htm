مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

DC Motor 25 حصان، 125 VDC, Arm current 171 A وستنجهاوس, ل Emergency lubricating oil pump of main turbine Westinghouse.

The first draft initiated by eng. Ahmed EL-husany, tested by eng. Tarek EL-kafafy, accepted by general director eng. Nady Mosaed, approved by eng. Mohamed EL-Tahawy Head sector of Shoubra EL-Kheima PS.

This document revised by eng. Nageh Soliman Shousha CEPC quality control director, and certified by eng. AfafEbrahim El Dib, CEPC inspection & quality control 's  head sector & MPIS coordinator.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Wear your safety helmet & safety shoes.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • مطلوب انقطاع الوحدة

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Megger 1000 V .

  • Avometer .

  • Thermal test element .

  • Vibration monitor .

  • Electrical toolbox .

  • DC clamp ammeter .

العمل المراد تنفيذه

Check the running condition

  1. Check operating current not to exceed Amp .

  2. Check winding temp. with a thermometer .

  3. تحقق من درجة حرارة تحمل. مع ميزان الحرارة.

  4. Check motor vibration

Stop the Motor and Isolate its CB

  1. Tighten loosen bolts.

  2. Check ground wire and tighten if necessary .

  3. Clean outside of motor and ventilation air inlets .

  4. Measure windings installation resistance not to be less than 15 MΩ .

  5. Inspect the motor interior if it very dirty disassemble the motor and clean .

  6. Wipe the commutator with a clean canvas .

  7. فحص مدى ملاءمة الفرش لل المبدل. إذا كان للمبدل سطح خشن، فيجب تنعيمه باستخدام أ قطعة من ورق الرمل

  8. Remove the inlet and outlet plugs and grease the bearings with (dolium-R grease) , then replace the inlet plug .

  9. Run the motor for 10 min before replacing the outlet grease plug

  10. Recheck the running condition.
