السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area

  • Wear your safety helmet, shoes, ... etc

  • Close the isolate valves for level glass

  • Wait level glass to be cold

    •

Tools and Equipment

  • Screw kit

  • Grinder & grinding wheel

  • Cleaning tools & keys kit

 

 

 

 

Work to be carried out

  1. الفحص الداخلي بحثًا عن أدلة على التآكل أو التحجيم

  2. Clean internal parts from the foreign bodies

  3. تحقق من استبدال الزجاج إذا كان به عيب

  4. Check gaskets change the defect

  5. Check the mica change the defect

  6. Tighten the bolts at cold

  7. قم بتدليك مستوى الزجاج ببطء عن طريق فتح الصمامات عند الحد الأدنى من التدفق

  8. Retighten the bolts after worming and make the level glass in service
