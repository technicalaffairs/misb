مقدمة

This document outlines the testing procedures P1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safty category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, safety belt ...etc.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • Fine cloth for cleaning purposes.

  • Digital multimeter, Megger (500 5000) volt

  • Air blower, Lubricant

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the bridge movement by traveling forward and backward for position low and high.

  2. Check the main trolley movement by traversing left and right for position low and high.

  3. Check the hoist up and down for position low and high.

  4. Check the hoist up and down for 2 speeds.

  • Check the limit switches of bridge limits at slow down and stop بواسطة السفر forward and backward.

  • تحقق من مفاتيح الحد للعربة الرئيسية لمعرفة الموضع slow down and stop by traversing left and right.

  • Check the limit switches for position stop for main trolley by hoisting up.

  • Check the limit switches for position slow down for main trolley by hoisting up slow down.

  • Check the limit switches for position stop for main trolley by hoisting down, up, down, and anti overlap.

  • Check the control of the crane with pedant that all operations is correct.

  • Check the control of signaling and lighting that all operations is correct.

  • Check the control of load test at 1.25 of total load.
