السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should be qualified and carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrrounding the work area is required.

  • ارتداء السلامة خوذة، أحذية.

Tools and Equipments

  • Safety helmet, shoes,

  • Oven Temperature tester.

  • Ohmmeter

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

Steps to Prepare for test

  1. مراجعة المواد المرجعية الفنية ودليل التصنيع.

  2. Obtain copy of the setting value of thermocouple and record at MRDS.

  3. افصل أسلاك RTD واعزل الأسلاك باستخدام الشريط.

  4. Remove RTD from thermowell and take it to lab.

Testing Procedure

  1. Put the RTD into the oven.

  2. Adjust the oven temperature to the correct value, wait 5 minutes using ohmmeter check if contact change its situation.

  3. If there is any error adjust the contact and repeat step 2 until reach the correct value.

  4. Repeat step 2 but for the other values of deg. C as stated in the MRDS.

Return RTD to service

  1. قم بإعادة RTD إلى الخدمة عن طريق إزالة أسلاك الأومتر من طرف RTD.

  2. Remove RTD from oven tester.

  3. Return the RTD back to its location.

  4. Connect the RTD wires.

  5. Place a copy of test result in the field maintenance file.
