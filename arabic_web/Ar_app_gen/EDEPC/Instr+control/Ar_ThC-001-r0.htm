السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should be qualified and carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrrounding the work area is required.

  • ارتداء السلامة خوذة، أحذية.

Tools and Equipments

  • Safety helmet, shoes,

  • درجة حرارة الفرن> 500 درجة. ج.

  • Voltmeter, mv ohmmeter.

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

خطوات الاستعداد للاختبار

  1. مراجعة المواد المرجعية الفنية ودليل التصنيع.

  2. Obtain copy of the setting value of thermocouple.                                      

  3. Disconnect thermocouple wires and isolate the wires using tape.

  4. يزيل thermocouple and take it to lab.

Testing Procedure

  1. Put the thermocouple into the oven and connect mv meter to thermocouple terminals.

  2. Adjust oven temp. to the ref. value, wait 5 minutes, سجل قراءة الفولتميتر وقارنها مع correct value.

  3. Repeat step 2 but for the other degrees C.

Return thermocouple  to Service

  1. Return thermocouple to service by removing the oven from the indicator vent.

  2. Close indicator vent.

  3. Open the manual isolating valve of indicator line.

  4. Place a copy of test result in the field maintenance file.
