السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should be qualified and carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrrounding the work area is required.

  • ارتداء السلامة خوذة، أحذية.

  • Close manual valve for Transmitter line.

Tools and Equipments

  • Safety helmet, shoes,

  • Digital Ammeter,

  • Pressure tester.

  • Adjustable open end Spanner.

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

Steps to Prepare for test

  1. مراجعة المواد المرجعية الفنية ودليل التصنيع.

  2. Obtain copy of setting values for transmitter and record it in the attachment MRDS.

  3. Remove transmitter indicator and connect the ammeter leads.

  4. Open high vent to atmosphere and connect the pressure tester to high vent of transmitter.

Testing Procedure

  1. Record the ammeter reading for 0 psi.

  2. Inject the suitable pressure bar using pressure tester, reference to setting values and MRDS and record the reading.

  3. Vent out the pressure and record the ammeter reading.

  4. Compare the ammeter reading with setting values, if there is any error repeat steps until reach the required reading.

Return transmitter to service

  1. Return transmitter to service by removing the ammeter leads from the indicator terminal and connect the indicator.

  2. Remove the pressure tester.

  3. Open the manual valve for transmitter line.

  4. Vent out air from transmitter lins.

  5. Close high vent.

  6. Place a copy of test result in the field maintenance file.
