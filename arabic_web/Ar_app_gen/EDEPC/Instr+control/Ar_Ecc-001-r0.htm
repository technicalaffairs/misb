مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

This test to inspect the eddy current transducer (five types) to measure shaft eccentricity,

The  first draft initiated by eng. Ashraf Farid, and tested by eng. Mona Galal, revised by EDEPC quality control eng. Abdl  Menem Al Emam, accepted by general manager eng. Aly Abd Elwhab, and approved by eng Fathy Mahmod Ragab: Head Sector of Talkha power station.

This document accepted by eng. Samir Abd El Gilil; EDEPC inspection & quality control general director, MPIS coordinator, and certified by accountant Mahmoud Soliman: EDEPC admin & financial inspection & quality control's general director, and supervisor of inspection & quality control's head sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح عمل ويجب أن يحمل موظف الصيانة فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير محيطة به منطقة العمل مطلوبة.

  • السلامة وصحية يجب استخدام المعدات (خوذة السلامة، أحذية السلامة، ...الخ).

  • Unit should be out of service.

    •

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Digital Multimeter type Fluke 85

  • Screw driver

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Measure resistance between coaxial cable and shield (fig .1) the  measuring value should be equal to (0.8/1.2  Ώ ) and  also the measuring value between  surface / coaxial cable, surface / shield  should be tends to ∞ Ω.

  2. If the measuring value in step 1 is not the value (0.8/1.2  Ω, ∞ Ω  ) then the transducer is defected and should be changed.
