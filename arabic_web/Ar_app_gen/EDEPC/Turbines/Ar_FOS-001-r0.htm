مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection check list I1, which must be performed every three months as indicated by M3 in the above title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Abdel Hamid Abu Zid, tested by eng. El-Sayed Mahmoud El-Kholy,  accepted by eng. Sawsan El lithe general manager, revised by eng. Zakaria El-Shahat EDEPC MPIS coordinator and approved by eng. Mohamed Hussein Gomaa quality insurance head sector.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the flow discharge and pressure of liquid fuel system.

  2. Check signals for the servo (I.C depart).

  3. If the liquid fuel components in the system is OK the hydraulic bypass valve and cylinder must be checked.

صيانة

  1. The tie rod nuts no need to loose.

  2. The piece piston construction eliminates for removing the piston rod and multi-lip piston.

  3. All parts removed from the cylinder that are to be reused should be thoroughly cleaned - be sure to carefully clean all cavities and grooves prior to replacing parts.

  4. Should be lightly lubricated with a clean lubricant (same type or compatible with, the fluid being used in the cylinder)

  5. Replace all static and moving seals if the cylinder is disassembled.
