مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed for fuel oil filter as indicated by Δ P = 0.6 bar or 150 operation hours in the above titled block.

The first draft is initiated by eng. Saad Samra, tested by eng. Mohamed Abdel Azeem at 01 July 2003, accepted by by General Manager eng. Mohamed El-Sarrag, revised by EDEPCo. quality control eng. Abouda El-Bana, and approved by eng. Moneer Mahmoud El-Wakeel Damietta CCPS Head Sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل و يجب أن يحمل موظفو الصيانة المؤهلون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work are is required..

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the filter mechanically by open inlet & outlet valves.

  • قم بإلغاء تهوية الفلتر عن طريق فتح صمام إزالة الهواء قبل فتحه.

  • Drain the filter by opening the drain valve

    • الأدوات والمعدات

    • Complete maintenance set.

    • Compressed air hose.

    • Cleaning articles (fluid, ragsetc).

    • Soft brushes.

    • Magnetic pick-up flexible.

    • Spray gun.

     

    العمل المراد تنفيذه

    1. Check differential pressure.

    2. Dismantle the filter cover.

    3. Pull off the filter to clean it.

    4. Clean the filter by solar.

    5. Replace the filter if it is required.

    6. Replace the gasket if it is required.

    7. Insert the filter in its correct position.

    8. Close the filter cover with gasket.

    9. Close the drain valve.

    10. Breath the air from the filter by opening the de-aerate valve, then close it.

    11. Open the inlet  & outlet valves.
