مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in above title block.

The turning gear used for turning the rotor of turbine with low speed during shutdown and reach its zero speed.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintenance stuff should be carrying the suitable safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • عزل قاطع الدائرة الكهربائية Turning gear motor.

  • افصل المحطات الكهربائية عن Turning gear motor.

  • Isolate the Turning gear mechanically by cutoff the input oil.

العمل المراد تنفيذه 

Turning Gear في العملية

  1. Measure vibrations level for bearings and compare it with the setting value, then analyze it and determine what to do.

  2. Check revolution of turbine rotor and compare it with the setting value then analyze it and determine what to do.

Turning Gear Out of Operation

  1. Measure vibrations level for bearings and compare it with the setting value, then analyze it and determine what to do (if it is needed).

  2. Check revolution of turbine rotor and compare it with the setting value then analyze it and determine what to do.

  3. Check bolts fixation, then re-tight it (if it is needed).

  4. Make test for bearings oil and add or change it (if it is needed).

  5. Check glands for leaking and replace it if it is needed.

  6. Check flanges for leaking.

  7. تحقق من Turning gear casing against rust and re-paint (if it is needed).

Attendance Checking during Operation

  1. Check vibration for turning gear

  2. Check lube oil pressure and level of oil tank.
