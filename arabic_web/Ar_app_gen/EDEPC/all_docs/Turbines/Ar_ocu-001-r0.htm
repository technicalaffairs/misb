Safety Precautions

  • Issue work order including the teamwork names

  • Use Safety fences (ropes) with caution marks to surround the work area.

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Be sure that the oil-cooling unit is out of service.

 Tools and Equipment

  • Complete maintenance tool set.

  • Fine brushes, Compressed air hose.

  • Special cleaning materials (trichoroethyline).

 

Works to be Carried out

  1. Check the air entry side at regular intervals.

  • Clean the cooling air entry side by using fine brush.

  • Blow out the cooler by compressed air or spray with a water jet in the opposite direction to the cooling airflow.

  • Be care to not distort the fins.

  • Use a team of jet blower with high wetting agents to clean and get rid of any dirts if the cooler has a layer of grease or oil. The jet should be in the opposite direction of the airflow. Dry the airside after cleaning.

  • Connect the oil side to a closed cleaning unit with forced circulation and filter unit; use trichoroethyline as a cleaning agent with 60C temperature.

  • Flushing should be done from both sides for 30 minutes approximately.

  • Quantity of water [150 300 L / min], pressure [3 4 bar].Check the air entry side at regular intervals.
