مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed for fuel oil transfer 9forward) pump doublex filter as indicated (when Δ P = 0.6 bar) in the above titled block

 

The first draft is initiated by eng. Saad Samra, tested by eng. Mohamed Abdel Azeem at 07 July 2003, accepted by by General Manager eng. Mohamed El-Sarrag, revised by EDEPCo. quality control eng. Abouda El-Bana, and approved by eng. Moneer Mahmoud El-Wakeel Damietta CCPS Head Sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • إصدار أمر العمل بما في ذلك أسماء العمل الجماعي.

  • Use Safety fences with caution marks to surround the work area.

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the filter mechanically by closing inlet & outlet valves.

  • قم بإلغاء تهوية الفلتر عن طريق فتح صمام إزالة الهواء قبل فتحه.

  • Drain the filter by opening the drain valve.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete maintenance set.

  • Compressed air hose.

  • Cleaning articles.

  • Soft brushes.

  • Magnetic pick-up flexible.

  • Spray gun.

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check differential pressure

  2. Dismantle the filter cover

  3. اسحب الفلتر لتنظيفه

  4. Clean the filter.

  5. Replace the filter if it is required

  6. Replace the gasket if it is required

  7. Insert the filter in its correct position

  8. أغلق غطاء الفلتر بحشية

  9. Close the drain valve

  10. Breath the air from the filter by opening the de-aerate valve, then close it

  11. Open the inlet & outlet valves
