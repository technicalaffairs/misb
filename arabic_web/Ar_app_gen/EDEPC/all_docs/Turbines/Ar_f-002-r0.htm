مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed for lube and control oil filter as indicated by I1 (when Δ P = 0.6 bar) in the above titled block.
 

The first draft is initiated by eng. Saad Samra, tested by eng. Mohamed Abdel Azeem at 28 June 2003, accepted by by General Manager eng. Mohamed El-Sarrag, revised by EDEPC quality control eng. Abouda El-Bana, and approved by eng. Moneer Mahmoud El-Wakeel Damietta CCPS Head Sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • Issue work order including the teamwork names.

  • Use Safety fences with caution marks to surround the work area is required.

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the filter mechanically by closing inlet & outlet valves.

  • قم بإلغاء تهوية الفلتر عن طريق فتح صمام إزالة الهواء قبل فتحه.

  • Drain the filter by opening the drain valve.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete maintenance set.

  • Compressed air hose.

  • Cleaning articles (fluid, ragsetc).

  • Soft brushes.

  • Magnetic pick-up flexible.

  • Spray gun.

 

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Dismantle the filter cover.

  2. Pull off the filter to clean it.

  3. Clean the filter.

  4. Replace the filter if it is required.

  5. Replace the gasket if it is required.

  6. Insert the filter in its correct position.

  7. Close the filter cover with gasket.

  8. Close the drain valve.

  9. Refill the oil filter with oil.

  10. Breath the air from the filter by opening the de-aerate valve, then close it.

  11. Open the inlet  & outlet valves.

  12. Check the leakage and treat it by re-tightening the filter if it is required.
