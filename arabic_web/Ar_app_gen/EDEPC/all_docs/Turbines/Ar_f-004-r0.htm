احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance stuff should be carry the suitable safety category,

    •

  • Use Safety fences with caution marks to surround the work area is required.

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the filter mechanically by closing inlet & outlet valves.

  • قم بإلغاء تهوية الفلتر عن طريق فتح صمام إزالة الهواء قبل فتحه.

  • Drain the filter by opening the drain valve.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete maintenance set.

  • Compressed air hose.

  • Cleaning articles.

  • Soft brushes.

  • Magnetic pick-up flexible.

  • Spray gun.

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. نصيحة سلة الشاشة وتنظيفها بالهواء المضغوط من الخارج والداخل.

  2. Remove any remaining dirts by a soft brush.

  3. Clean the filter casing by a no-fluff cloth like a leather cloth.

  4. Reinstall the cleaned screen basket, the spring of the screen must be about 30 - 40 mm above the casing flange.

  5. Check the tide seal (replace it if it is required).

  6. Tight the screws of the filter chamber.

  7. Do up venting screw to deaerate the strainer.

  8. Return the fuel gas filter in the service again.
