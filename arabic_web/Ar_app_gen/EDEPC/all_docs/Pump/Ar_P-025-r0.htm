مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed every six months or 4000 operating hours which comes first as indicated by M6 / H 4000 in the title block

The first draft initiated by eng. Mohamed Mohamed Helaly Abd Alkarem and tested by eng. Mahmoud Mohamed Zayed and eng Tarq Mohamed Rashad Ahmed at 18 Jan 2004, accepted by general director eng. Gaber Desoqy Mostafa, and approved by eng. Mohamed Mahdey Rezq Alnshar Head Sector of Sidi Kirir 1&2 Power Station.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة الأمان المناسبة للهوية.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc

    • The motor have to be stopped and its breaker isolated before starting any maintenance procedure

أدوات والمعدات

  • Lubricating oil type: Mobil DTE Heavy medium - ISO - VG 68 ( or equivalent) approximately 103 Liters (77 for motor thrust, 26 for Pump thrust) see Note 1

  • Grease type: Mobilex EB3 (or iithium base equivalent Mobil-PLX 47) approximately 350 gm for regressing see Note 2

  • Packing type: CF-P, 4 pieces (20 mm cross section - length 336.3)

  • Grease Gun with - capacity 5 kg for changing grease

  • Grease Gun with - capacity 1 kg for Re-greasing

  • وجع عزم الدوران

  • Adjustable wrenches - with different sizes

  • Wrenches with width size (30,24,20,16)

  • Mechanical tool kit

ملاحظة 1: يجب تغيير الزيت المحمل للمحرك والمضخة كل مرة 4000 ساعة تشغيل (6 أشهر)

Note 2: for motor lower bearing grease should be changed every 2000 running hour (3 months)

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

تحضير

  Electrical Isolation

  1. Open main motor breaker, tag no. 1NB0206

  2. Turn off DC supply

  3. Open space heater No. 1NHA-12A-CCH-25

Mechanical Isolation

  1. Close suction valve No. 1AD-V-021

  2. Close discharge valve No. 1AD-V-027

  3. Close إعادة تدوير valve No. 1AD-V-136

  4. Close discharge vent valve No. 1AD-V-025

  5. Close seal water valve No. 1AD-V-030

  6. Close pump cooling water valve No. 1EB-V-112 & 113

  7. Close motor cooling water valve No. 1EB-V-114 & 115

  8. فتح صمام الصرف رقم 1AD-V-118

Procedure(s)

MOTOR      type ANV-WT001,  mfg. TECO

  1. Upper Bearing type - RENK U - 70 (motor thrust Br.)

  2. Check for oil color, quality and Taking sample for analysis Change if required

  3. Check for oil level. Top up if required.

  4. Lower Bearing type - NU 238

  5. Check for grease quality and color, change if required

  6. Flush and clean the oil cooler if required. Flushing water pressure < 6 bar

  7. التنظيف العام والفحص لخطوط توصيل مياه التبريد والزيت التسرب (تشديد إذا لزم الأمر)

PUMP       type WKTA300/6, mfg. KSB

Pump Thrust Bearing

  1. Check for oil color, quality and take a sample for analysis, change if required

  2. Check for oil level and top up if required to the middle of side glass

  3. Flush and clean the oil cooler if required. Flushing water pressure < 6 ber

  4. General cleaning and inspection for cooling water and oil connection lines for leakage (tighten if required)

صندوق حشو المضخة وأكمام العمود

 Check backing and change if required.

  • إحكام ربط الغدة (M 16 Bolts) إذا لزم الأمر أو إحكام تركيبها باليد فقط

    • Checking Tightened parts  (Check tightening torque between)

    1. Motor and motor stool.

    2. البراز الحركي و intermediate lantern.

    3. Intermediate lantern, discharge cover, and discharge elbow.

    4. Discharge elbow and foundation ring.

    5. Discharge elbow and column pipe.

    6. Upper and lower halves of coupling.
