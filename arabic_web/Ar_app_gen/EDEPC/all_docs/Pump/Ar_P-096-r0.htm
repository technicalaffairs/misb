احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح عمل، والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear your safety equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask, safety glass .. etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor.

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor.

  • The pump must be isolated mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves.

  • Be sure that the pump must be completely drainage.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Cleaning fluid. Manual puller

  • Feeler gauge, Dial indicator

  • Vibrometer, Straight edge, Rags

 

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

Pump in Service

  1. Check discharge quantity and pressure then, compare it with the setting valve

  2. Measure vibration level for bearings and compare it with the setting valve of bearing and seal box

  3. Check of mechanical seal              

  4. تحمل الشحوم

  5. Check the temperature

Pump out of Service

  1. Check Alignment between ( Motor pump ) 

  2. Check bolts fixation, then re tightens it ( if it is needed )

  3. Check discharge, pressure and compare it with the setting value then replace the worn out components  if it is needed .

  4. Check mechanical seal leakage

  5. Check flanges for leaking

  6. Check the pump casing against rust and re paint  if it is needed.
