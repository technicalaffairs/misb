مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, which must be carried out every six months as indicated by M6  in the above title block

The pump function is to cool the outlet steam from LP turbine to convert the steam to condensate water, and also to cool the closed cooling water for accessories

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح عمل، والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear your safety equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask, safety glass .. etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor.

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor.

  • The pump must be isolated mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves.

  • Be sure pump must be completely drainage.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • سائل التنظيف، ساحب يدوي

  • Feeler gauge, Dial indicator

  • Vibrometer, Straight edge, Rags

 

 

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

 

Pump in Service

  1. Check discharge quantity and pressure then, compare it with the setting value

  2. Measure vibration level for bearings and compare it with the setting value , then analyze it and determine what to do ( if it is needed )

  3. Check the pressure of oil and temperature of the cooling water

  4. Check gland leakage

Pump out of Service

  1. Clean the oil cooler

  2. تحقق من خطوط الصلب اقتران

  3. تحقق من المحاذاة وقارنها بقيمة الإعداد

  4. Check flanges for water leakage

  5. Check bolts fixation, then retightens it ( if it is needed )

  6. Check the packing of the gland.

  7. Analyze the oil and refill if it is necessary

  8. افحص غلاف المضخة ضد الصدأ وأعد طلاءه (إذا لزم الأمر)
