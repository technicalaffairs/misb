مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block

The technical specification of the pump CCCP centrifugal
Q = 360 M3 / Hr        H = 54 M        P = 75 kv        V = 400 volt

The first draft initiated and tested by eng.Samy Shehata Ebrahim, accepted by eng.  Hanafy Fahmy Madny general director, approved by eng. Abd El Kerim Abu Zeid Ismail Head Sector of High Dam Hydraulic Power Station, and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem MPIS HPGC Coordinator.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintanance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes.... إلخ

  • Stop pump and switch off the power supply

  • Close the out let and inlet valves

Tools and Equipment

  • Set of wrench, alignment equipment

  • Grease for lubricating

  • Cleaning material

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the packing of the gland of the pump add or change

  2. Check the packing of the gland of the outlet and inlet valves

  3. Check the bolts of the pump and motor

  4. Check the alignment of the pump

  5. Operate the pump and check the noise and bearing

  6. Check the current
