احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipments as safety healthy, safety shoes, ...etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor.

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor.

  • The pump must be isolated mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves.

  • Be sure pump must be completely drainage.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة.

  • Cleaning fluid, rags, suitable lifting device

  • Manual Puller, Feeler Gauges

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

Pump in Service

  1. Check any noise for vibration.

  2. Check any leakage from the piping connection to pump.

  3. Check the leakage from stuffing box and prevented it by re tighten the gland bolts.

  4. Check leakage of lubricating oil from bearing house.

  5. Check the oil level.

  6. Check the pressure in pump delivery.

  7. Check the temperature of bearing house.

After Isolating the Pump (Mechanically & Elect.)

  1. Check the coupling pins.

  2. Change the packing if there is more leakage.

  3. Prevent the leakage of lubricating oil.

  4. Add lubricating oil to pump of the level less than 2/3 side glass.

  5. Retighten the bolts connected pump and motor with foundation.
