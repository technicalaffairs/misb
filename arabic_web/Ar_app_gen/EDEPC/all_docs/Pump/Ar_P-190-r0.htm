احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية لتطويق منطقة العمل is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker for the pump motor.

  • The pump must be isolated mechanically by closing suction and delivery valves.

  • Unit out of service.

 

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • سائل التنظيف، Soft brush

  • جهاز رفع مناسب، مقياس الشعور

  • Hammer, Manual puller

  • Dial indicator

  • وجع عزم الدوران

العمل المراد تنفيذه

 

  1. Check the main bearing temperature.

  2. Check the pump for smooth operation.

  3. Check all anchor bolts and support steel fasteners bolts, and tighten if necessary.

  4. Check pump packing leakage and adjust as required.

  5. Check pump / drive alignment.

  6. تحقق حالة صمام تخفيف الضغط.

قم بالخطوات التالية

  1. يفحص pump housing for signs of wear.

  2. يفحص bearings and couplings and make adjustments or replacements where necessary. Give particular attention to bearing seals.

  3. Re-grease pump bearing with Mobilux 2 ( 0.2 KG )

  4. تحقق من تعبئة المضخة واستبدلها كما هو مطلوب.

  5. Check bearing seals and replace as required.

  6. Tighten all hold down bolts in pump housing.

Maintenance Instruction

 

Lubrication

  1. Grease timing gears routinely when operating continuously

  2. Grease bearings at the same time as timing gears in step (A)
    Note: Zerk fitting do not have ball detente. This prevents bearings from
    الإفراط في التشحيم. قد ينفد الشحوم الزائدة من تركيب الزرك عندما
    pump is in operation

Packing

  1. Use "John crane 1065 packing or equal" size required is 5/16 square.
    This packing gives: excellent wear, high chemical resistance great antifrictional value.

مضخة تعليمات إزالة التغليف وإعادة التعبئة

   

التفكيك

  1. Remove packing nuts (19A and 19B-see fig A, pg. 7)
    ملحوظة: some pumps have a lock screw to secure the packing nut once it has been adjusted

  2. Use a packing pick to remove the old packing. Care must be used not to scratch or damage the shaft in the packing area while removing and installing packing

  3. Inspect the packing gland area. Be sure it is clean

Re-packing Procedure

  1. Select the desired packing. See step # 2 (A) above. Pre-formed packing will seat in more quickly

  2. Lubricate your packing rigs with "Triflon" or use a good grade of machine oil if "Triflon" is not available

  3. Insert the packing rings.
    ملحوظة : rotate each new packing ring so the split is 90 degrees away from the last split. A small screw driver can be used to press the packings into the gland area. Be careful not to damage or scratch the shaft

  4. Leave approximately 1/4 of threads clear of the last packing so that the adjusting nut (19 A and 19B) can be started

  5. Tighten the packing adjusting nuts (19 A and 19 B) a little more than "finger tight"

  6. Start pump up and adjust the packings على النحو التالي:

    1. تشديد العبوات حتى يتم إبطاء التسرب إلى تيار صغير عند كل منهما التعبئة. اترك المضخة تعمل لمدة ساعتين تقريبًا لبدء عملية الضخ عملية الجلوس. ملحوظة : if the packings are over tightened so that no leakage occurs, the packing will burn and score the shaft. Always allow a little pump age to weep through the packing to insure cooling.

    2. بعد ساعتين من أ قد يحدث تسرب سريع للسائل من خلال العبوة أعد تشديد العبوة إلى التنقيط البطيء. السماح لمدة 15 دقيقة على الأقل ل عبوات لتشكيل العمود في منطقة التعبئة.  

    3. Any further packing adjustment must be done with extreme care. Tighten the packing nut 1/8 turn, then immediately loosen the packing nut 1/16 turn. Are the packings still weeping a little moisture? (They should be). Allow the pump to run several hours at this setting

    4. For any subsequent packing adjustment, follow step 6c above and be aware of the burn comment made in step 6a
