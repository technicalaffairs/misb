مقدمة

توضح هذه الوثيقة equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on a monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the titled block

The Traveling water screen system is used for cleaning of the Nile River water utilized for the operation of the combined cycle power station from plastics, wood .... etc

The first draft initiated by eng. Mohamed Abd El Azeem, tested by eng. Mohamed Fawzy Agwa at 13 July 2004, revised by EDEPC quality control eng Abouda Elbanna, accepted by general manager eng. Mohamed El Sarrag, approved by eng Moneer Mahmoud Al Wakeel; Damietta power station head sector, and certified by eng. Aly Al Lethy: EDEPCo inspection and quality control head sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should be carry the Suitable safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

    •

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the screen mechanically and electrically.  

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Maintenance tool set.

  • Grease (multi purpose grease).

  • Wires to raise up the screen cover.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Grease the motor roller bearing.

  2. Grease the sliding roller bearings.

  3. Grease the sliding roller pins.
