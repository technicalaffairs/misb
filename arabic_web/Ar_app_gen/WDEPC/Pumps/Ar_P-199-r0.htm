مقدمة

This document outlines equipment inspection I2, which must be preformed every 3000 working hours as indicated by H3000 in the title block.  (3000HS, 1EA-P001A)

The pump supplier KSB Germany type SNW 600-719

The motor type: ANVE-WT001, supplier: Teco electric & machinery Co., LTD Taiwan,

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل.

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution works surrounding the work area is required

  • ارتداء معدات السلامة والصحة مثل خوذة السلامة وأحذية السلامة ... الخ

  • Isolate the pump mechanically and the motor electrically

  • Be sure the motor of the pump is stopped and its breaker isolated before starting any maintenance.

  • Electrical Isolation:
    - Circuit breaker 1NB011 is opened
    - Switch off the DC Supply
    - Space heater  9NHA 31A- ICH-26 is open

  • Mechanical Isolation:
    - Close the discharge valve 1EA-V001A

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Hydraulic Jack

  • Screw Drivers set

  • Hydraulic torque wrench

  • Normal Key set

  • Special Key set

  • Allen Key set

  • Nut Key set

  • Alignment tools

  • Oil for pump thrust bearing approximate 15 L (Mobile oil heavy medium) ISO-VG68

  • Grease for Motor Lower bearing and coupling: (no. of  bearing NU224, grease Mobil plex 47: 120g

  • Oil for Motor Upper bearing approximate 60 L (Mobile oil heavy medium) ISO-VG68

  • Packing qty, dimension, type: 4pieces, 95x127x16mm, RT-P

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the rubber (8 pieces, no. 315  coupling & alignment coupling (Air gap:3-8mm, radial:0.468, axial:;0.468)

  2. Check the oil (viscosity, acid no., contamination) for thrust bearings (pump and motor) and change if required

  3. Ensure the grease Mobile plex 47 for lower bearing of motor and change if required.

  4. Check the tighten for all bolts of the pump and motor.

  5. Clean the cooling water pipes of thrust bearing (pump and motor)

  6. Check all valves (drains, vent, ... etc)

  7. Generally clean the external pump body

  8. Check the packing ( 4 pieces, 95x127x16mm, RT-P) and shaft protecting sleeve and change if required.
