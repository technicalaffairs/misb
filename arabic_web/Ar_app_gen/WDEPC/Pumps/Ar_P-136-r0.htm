مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every 1500 operating hours as indicated by H1500 in the title block.

The pump function is provided with the boilers required fuel and with required pressure.

العمل المراد تنفيذه
مضخة الماء الحلو

  1. Uncouple the motor from the pump by removing the coupling bolts.

  2. فحص وتسجيل المحاذاة والتخليص المحوري.

  3. Drain the oil from thrust bearing.

  4. Inspect and clean the oil cooler.

  5. Inspect the thrust bearing.

  6. Inspect and clean the cooling water tubes.

  7. Inspect and repack the gland with new packing if required.

  8. Realign the motor to the pump.

  9. Reconnect the coupling bolts.
