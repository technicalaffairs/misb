مقدمة

This document outlines equipment inspection I1, to be preformed every six months bases as indicated by M6 in the title block.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued.

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • The motor have to be stopped and its breaker isolated before starting any maintenance procedure.

Electrical isolation

  • Open the Circuit Breaker of 2 CU1 pump A,

العزلة الميكانيكية

  • Close the suction valve 9AR-V-B-06A-1

  • Close the discharge valve 9 AR-V-B-08A-1

  • Close the manual recirculation valve 9 AR-V-B-128A-1

  • Close the cooling valve 9 AR-V-CW-6A-1 

Tools and Equipment

  • Hydraulic jack

  • Screw drivers set, Normal key set

  • Hydraulic torque wrench

  • Special key set, Alignment tools

  • Allen key set, Nut key set

  • Oil quantity (1.67 liter/3000hr) & type (mobil 46)

  • Grease type (AB1)

 

Work to be Carried Out

  1. Check suction & discharge pressure.

  2. Check current, flow rate liquid temperature & bearing temperature.

  3. Check leakage from shaft seal.

  4. Exchange of bearing lube oil.

  5. Greasing the coupling.

  6. Check alignment of pump & motor.

  7. Check coupling rubber for wear.
