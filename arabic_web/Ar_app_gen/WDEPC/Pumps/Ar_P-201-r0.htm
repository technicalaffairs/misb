مقدمة

This document outlines equipment inspection I2, to be preformed every 8000 working hours as indicated by H8000 in the title block.

The motor of the pump: type ANVE - WT 001, Teco Electric & Machinery Co. LTD Taiwan

Safety Precautions

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • The motor have to be stopped and its breaker isolated before starting any maintenance procedure

Tools and Equipment

  • Hydraulic jack

  • Screw drivers set

  • Hydraulic torque wrench

  • Normal key set

  • Special key set

  • Allen key set

  • Nut key set

  • Alignment tools

  • Oil quantity & type:
    Pump thrust bearing  : oil approximate 40 L oil (mobil DTE light) ISO - VG 32
    Motor lower bearing : bearing No. (NU 226)
    Motor lower bearing grease : mobilux 3 (140 g)
    Compling grease : mobilux EP 004 (0.86 dm3)
    Motor upper bearing : oil approximate 60 L oil (mobil oil heavy medium) ISO-VG 68

  • Packing Quantity dimension, and type: (6 pieces - 10mm - LATT -YFLON 4788)

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the (teeth, gear seals, sealing faces) coupling and alignment coupling (air gap:40 (tolerance +2,-5)mm, radial:0.56, axial 0.22)

  2. Change the oil for motor upper bearing (oil approximate 60L oil - mobil oil heavy medium ISO - VG 68).

  3. Change the oil for pump thrust bearing (oil approximate 40L oil- mobil DTE light ISOVG 32).

  4. Change of the grease mobilux 3 (140g) for lower bearing of motor.

  5. Change of the grease mobilux EP 004 (0.86 dm3) for coupling.

  6. Check the tighten of all bolts for the pump and motor.

  7. Clean the cooling water pipes of thrust bearings (pump and motor)

  8. Check the goodness of inlet outlet sealing water drain and vent valves.

  9. General cleaning for the external pump body.

  10. Check the packing (6pieces - 10mm - LATT, YFLON 4788) lantern ring shaft protecting sleeve and change if required

  11. Check the cooling for the packing.
