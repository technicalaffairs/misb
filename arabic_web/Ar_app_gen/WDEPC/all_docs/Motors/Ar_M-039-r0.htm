السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes, breathing-mask, safety glasses is required.

  • The motor must be out of service.

  • Circuit breaker in test position.

أدوات والمعدات

  • Electrical equipment kit.

  • Injection test set.

  • Dry Cloth.

Work to be carried out

  1. Clean air - ducts

  2. Ensure that all fixing bolts are secured .

  3. Ensure that electrical connection are tightened .

  4. Check the isolators and clean it .

  5. Check the oil level

  6. Check operation of the motor from local and remote operation on test position.

  7. Measure winding insulation resistance.

  8. Check all protection devices of the motor by protection group.
