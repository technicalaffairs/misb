مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1 for unit 5, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

The first draft is initiated by eng. Emad Mohamed El-Tohamy, tested by eng. Sawsan Elsaid Ali, reviewed & accepted by eng. Hanaa Nasr Abd El-Gawad general manager, approved by eng Aly Anor El-Faumy, and certified by eng. Mona Ahmed Al Sherqawy: WDEPC consultant engineer "A" and MPIS Coordinator.

احتياطات السلامة

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

Boiler Circulating Pump Body Temperature

  1. Check the TSHH 101A "temperature switch very high", from thermocouple TE 101A, range: 0 to 100 C, high threshold 60C MZ value 0.60, LO (14) = 1(trip + start forbidden)

  2. Check the TSH 101 B "temperature switch high", from thermocouple TE 101B, Range: 0 to 100C, high treshold 55C, MZ value 0.55, LO (22) = 1(alarm)

  3. تحقق من مفتاح درجة الحرارة التفاضلي TDSH 102 (المضخة جسم الشفط والمضخة"، من المزدوج الحراري TE 102 A & TE 102C، النطاق: 0 إلى 400 ج، عتبة عالية 56 درجة مئوية، قيمة MZ 0.56، LO 17 = (1) (البدء ممنوع)، القيم في Micro Z cub باستخدام وحدة تحكم micro Z

  4. Check the FLAPDSLL 001 ""pressure differential switch very low", from PDL 001, range :0 to 3 bar MZ value 0.58 bar, LO (33) = 0(trip)

  5. Check FLAPDSL 001, from PDT 001, range: 0 to 3 bar, MZ value 1.12 bar trip, LO (12) = 1(alarm)

  6. Check PDT 001 "pressure differential transmitter (upper drum, lower drum", differential pressure between suction manifold and lower drum

    0% 0 bar 4 mA
    20% 0.60 bar 7.2 مللي أمبير
    40% 1.20 بار 10.4 مللي أمبير
    60% 1.80 bar 13.6mA
    80% 2.4 bar 16.8mA
    100% 3 bar 20mA

  7. Ensure that the two hand valves (HVC 001, HVC002) open to obtain pressure equal to (1.9 bar 1.8 bar) at start up.

Signals appear at MZ cub

  • Lo .33 PDSLL 001 A

  • Lo .12 PDSL 001

  • Lo. 034 PDSLL 002 A

  • Lo .13 PDSL 002
