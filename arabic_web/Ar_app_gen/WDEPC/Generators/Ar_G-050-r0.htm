مقدمة

This document outlines the maintenance inspection on 3 Ph, Turbo Alternator which must be performed  monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the title block, This procedure was applied in Damanhour Thermal Power Plant3*65mw since 1976,The generator data: 81.25MVA, 10.5KV, 4.45KA, PF=0.8, 50HZ, 3000 rpm, Y connection .

The first draft initiated by eng. Mohamed Fatehy AboElkher, tested by eng.Samir Ouf Hassoub , accepted by general manager eng. Hamdy Dorgam, approved by eng. Gaber Mohamed Farahat head sector of Damanhour power station, and certified by eng. Sania Diab Attia Diab WDEPC inspection head sector and MPIS coordinator.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • Work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should the suitable safety  category.

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes, gloves ... etc.

  • Gas turbine out of service at cold state.

الأدوات و المعدات

  • Complete tool set

  • Safety helmet safety shoes, breathing mask safety glass... etc

  • New brushes

  • Digital voltmeter, and clamp ammeter

Work to be carried out

Seal Oil Supply System

Check  oil filters and cleaning if required .

Coolers

Check  vents .

Hydrogen System

  1. Check  gas consumption .

  2. Inspect  dry type filters and cleaning if required .

Exciter Set

  1. Inspect  air filters and clearing if required .

  2. Inspect  brushes .

Hydrogen System

  1. Check  gas drier and clearing if  required .

  2. Check  thermal gas analyzer

Rotor

Inspect sliping brushes

Bearing

  1. Inspect brains at oil vapour exfractor and pressure pipes

  2. Inspect bearing insulation

  3. Inspect oil vapour extractor fan

Exciter

Check bearing insulation

Shaft seats

Check oil level controllers and alarm switches
