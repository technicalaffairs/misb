مقدمة

This document outlines the condenser inspection which must be performed every 4000 operations hours.

The maintenance procedures aim to guarantee the perfect efficiency of the components involved in the condensing system which should be strictly observed. As a matter of fact a decrease in efficiency or failure of any of these units will adversely effect the condenser performance.

The Condenser Technical Specification

Number of condenser :2                            Total length of pipes : 7.5m

Number of pipes in each condenser : 6800 pcs

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area

  • Wear safety and healthy equipments as safety healthy, shoes, ...etc.

العمل ليكون نفذت 

  1. Be sure the pipeline is drained

  2. Open condenser bolt

  3. Inspect gasket and replace with a new gasket if necessary

  4. Clean tube condenser by source pressurized water jet

  5. Inspect tube leakage by pressurized the shell and blocked any tube leaks

  6. Close condenser
