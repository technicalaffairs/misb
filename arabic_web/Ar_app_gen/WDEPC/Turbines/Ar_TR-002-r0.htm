مقدمة

 

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I2, to be performed on yearly bases as indicated by Y1 in the above title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Ragab Goada Abo-Zeed tested by eng. Emad Abraheem Gooda, accepted by general manager eng. Hassan Saeed Dosoki , approved by eng. السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued

  • The maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير مطلوب المحيطة بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, shoes gloves ... etc

  • Shutdown the trash rack

  • Circuit breaker of trash rack racked out

Tools and Equipment

  • Complete maintenance tool set

  • Cleaning fluid, rags

Work to be carried out

  1. Greasing of the rope (Mobil arma 789)

  2. Greasing the bearing supports (Mobil plex 47)

  3. Greasing the push pulleys (Mobil plex 47)

  4. Inspect the oil of the hydraulic unit & reduction gearbox

  5. Greasing the springs (Mobil arma 789)
