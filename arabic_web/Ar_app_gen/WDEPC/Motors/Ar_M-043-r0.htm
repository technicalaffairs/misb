مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

The first draft is initiated by eng. Emad Mohamed El-Tohamy, tested by eng. Sawsan Elsaid Ali, reviewed by eng. Ahmed Mohamed Aly Moussa, accepted by eng. Mohamed Abd El-Sadik Ahmed: general manager, approved by eng Aly Anor El-Faumy, and certified by eng. Mona Ahmed Al Sherqawy: WDEPC consultant engineer "A" and MPIS Coordinator.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued.

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, etc.

  • Switching of the 380V CB of the pump suction valve and rack it out and lock it.

  • Main CB 6kv off and rack it out and and lock it

  • 6kv Ground switch on

Tools and Equipment

  • Megger, Avometer.

  • Air compressor for cleaning.

  • Brushes.

Work to be Carried Out

  1. General cleaning.

  2. Check the earth cable tightening.

  3. Check the motor connection tightening.

  4. Check the protection devices.

  5. Measure the insulation of the motor.

  6. Check the stator temperature.
