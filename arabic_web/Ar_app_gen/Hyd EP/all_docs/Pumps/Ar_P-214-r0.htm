مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The screw pump type is self priming positive displacement pump and is used for delivery of lubricating fluids. veyor elements are three spindles, each with a double pitch or circulating the lubricating oil two separate filling oil pump sets are provided one pump is driven by an AC motor during normal operation conditions. The second pump is equipped with DC motor for standby.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued.

  • The qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety helmet, shoes .... etc.

  • The pump is out of service.

  • The motor is disconnected from the control panel.

Tools and Equipment

  • Complete tool set includes manual wrenches ... etc.

  • Some mechanical spare parts (mechanical seal bearing ... etc).

  • During pushing on of the mechanical seal assembly sleeve.

  • Tighten hexagonal screws with a torque wrench.

  • Blanco 1 ton.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

Every 500 working hours

  1. Check pump sound.

 

Every 3 to 5 Years

  1. Disassemble the pump.

  2. Check all internal parts for wear.

  3. Check for perfect surface - runing path of the roller bearing.
