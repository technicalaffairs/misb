مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

This pump function to dewater the drainage water in the pit.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Maintenance staff should will trained & qualified or directly supervised by some one

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area are required

  • Safety and healthy equipment must be used as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • The pit should be dewatered.

Tools and Equipment

  • Safety and healthy equipment

  • Torque wrenches,

  • Allen key, screw driver

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check that the guide bars are vertical

  2. Change the oil (Misr 37)

  3. Change the oil method: Lay the pump on the its side over two supports.

  4. Remove the screw oil and its O ring from one of the oil holes.

  5. Turn the pump so that the oil hole faces downwards and remove the other oil hole screw.

  6. Replace or fix worn and damaged parts.

  7. Replacing the impeller: worn impeller often have very sharp edges and to replace it; remove nuts, impeller screw, and remove the washer then pull off the impeller.

  8. Installing the impeller: make sure that the end of the shaft is clean and free of burns, polish-off any flaws.

  9. Clean and oil all surfaces and O rings.

  10. Check the key is seated in the key way on the shaft, grease end of the shaft and impeller hub, place washer on the  impeller screw, press the impeller on to the shaft with impeller screw and tight the screw

  11. Check that the impeller is firmly seated and rotated by hand.
