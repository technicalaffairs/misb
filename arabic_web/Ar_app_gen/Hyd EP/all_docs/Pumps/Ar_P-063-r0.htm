مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection  I2, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The technical specification of the pump power 0.37 kw-400v-50Hz

The first draft initiated & tested by eng. Abd El Mohsen Mohammed general manager of  Nag Hammadi Hyd. PS,  and certified by by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem HPGC  inspection and quality control general director

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear the appropriate safety and healthy equipment as safety shoes, helmet, قناع التنفس، نظارات السلامة,

    •

  • Stop pump and switch off the power supply

  • Close the outlet and inlet valves

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet and safety shoes

  • Set of wrenches, Cleaning material

  • Alignment equipment

  • Grease for lubricating

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the alignment of the pump.

  2. Operate the pump to check the noise.

  3. Check the key and bearing and change if need.

  4. Check the time operate and stopping time (3 min).

  5. Operate the pump and check the load current.
