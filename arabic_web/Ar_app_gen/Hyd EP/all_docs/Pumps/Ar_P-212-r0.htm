مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The two pumps supply the accumulator by the pressure oil.

The fitting location of the pump modules on the oil tank is freely selectable in case of failure the pump on modules can be exchange during operation the pump modules are fitted vertically pumps suction return and leakage pipes are in the oil tank where as all return and leakage oil are below the oil level, motors pressure limiting valves minimizes connections and cocks are above oil tank.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued.

  • The maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, gloves, ... etc.

  • The unit must be out of service (at monthly, annually, overhaul, capital maintenance)

  • The motor must be disconnected.

Tools and Equipment

  • Complete tool set includes manual wrenches ... etc.

  • Some special tools (mechanical seal - ball bearing - etc).

  • Blanco 1 ton

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the check valve - unloaded valve - adjustable pressure limiting valve

  2. Check the suction strainer and clean

  3. Check the ball bearing and mechanical seal

  4. Check the direction of rotation
