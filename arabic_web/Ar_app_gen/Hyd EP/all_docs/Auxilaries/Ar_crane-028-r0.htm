مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

ال المواصفات الفنية للرافعة الحمولة القصوى 175 طن.

The first draft initiated & tested by eng. Mohamed Abdel Wahab El Darder, accepted by general director eng. Nagy Nageeb Saleh and approved by eng. Mohamed Abdel Baset head sector of Aswan dam (1&2) hydraulic power stations and certified by eng. Kamel Raghib Hakem HPGC MPIS coordinator

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل

  • الصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة الأمان المناسبة للهوية

  • Wear the personal safety and healthy protective equipment as safety shoes, helmet, ...etc

  • The crane must be out of service

  • Switch off the power supply

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete tool set

  • Cleaning materials (dry cloth - benzene - grease for bearing)

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Measure and record the insulation resistance for motors

  2. Clean commutator, slip ring, brushes for motors

  3. Check the ball bearings of the motors, grease / lubricate or replace if needed, (bearings and grease must be kept free of dirt)

  4. Clean and inspect the main contacts of the contactors

  5. Inspect and check the brake of the motors

  6. Check all the tightens of the power and control circuits connections

  7. Connect the power source to the crane and check all movements
