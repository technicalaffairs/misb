 Return and filtering module

مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

The return and filtering system is standardized and based on a modular design. The exact fitting location on the oil tank module is freely selectable

The oil tank is installed above the turbine. The leakage oil coming from the turbine returns to the turbine. The leakage oil coming from the turbine returns to the lower leakage tank pump provides oil flow through the return filter and back to the oil tank the oil return from servo motor directly back to the oil tank

Filtering of leakage oil, control of the filters and control of the oil.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل.

  • يجب أن يحمل موظفو الصيانة فئة الأمان المناسبة للهوية.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, gloves, ... etc.

  • The unit must be out of service.

    •

  • The leakge oil tank should be emply from oil (overhaul capital maintenance annually)

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete tool set includes manual wrenches ... etc

  • DC lamp 36V.

  • Cleaning rags.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the leakege oil pump and clean the suction strainer.

  2. Change the double oil filters.

  3. Check that no oil leakage from tank, on a monthly maintenance clean the tank from outside, and on an annually maintenance clean the tank from inside.
