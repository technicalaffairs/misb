مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be carried out on a monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the above title block.

The  function of the gate shaft water level indicator is to show the level of water inside the gate shaft.

The first draft was initiated and tested by eng. Kamal Amin Gad Allah, accepted by general director eng. Atty سيفين رزق الله, approved by eng. Mohamed Abdel-Baset head sector of Aswan Dam (1&2) power stations and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem HPGC  inspection and quality control general director.
