مقدمة

This document out line the equipment inspection check list I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The technical specification of the lighting (lamp 40, 20 w), lamp 100,150,200 voltage 220 v, 36 v

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, shoes... etc

  • Switch off the AC current

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety and healthy equipment

  • Cleaning material (rag, cloth)

  • Ladder

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check the lamps in every level for station

  2. فحص المقاولين للخروج من المصابيح في الطابق 118، 112، 115، 139، 143، 160

  3. Change the (automatic, lamps, starters, trans) if you need
