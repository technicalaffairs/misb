مقدمة

This document outline the equipment inspection checklist I3, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

Turbine bearing is the bearing which carry the weight of all running parts of the unit and carry the vertical water force component.

Thrust bearing located up to the main bearing and it is carried  by the turbine cover.

This document shows the protective maintenance which must be performed to discover any oil leakage and check that all parts are تشديد ويكونون في أماكنهم.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • Maintenance staff should be will trained and qualified or directly supervised by some one who is.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • The unit must be stopped and the break on

  • The oil circulating pumps must be off

  • Gate lock and break must be on

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety and healthy equipment.

  • Wrenches.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Monitor oil condition when a change of oil is necessary.

  2. Clean the entire system properly.

  3. Filter the new oil to 70 microns or better before it is put into the oil system.

  4. Check the thermo signal bulb if it is in its place or not.

  5. Check the tightening of all visual parts into the bearing.

  6. Change the coupling rubber.

  7. Clean the strainer of the oil.

  8. Clean the heat exchanger by reversing the pass of the water circulation.
