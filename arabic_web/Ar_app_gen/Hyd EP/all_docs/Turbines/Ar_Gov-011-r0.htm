مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

Content and function of GA module:

Pressure vessel: to ensure maintenance of the necessary oil pressure oil system.

Pressure relife valve: to protect pressure vessel.

Air refill valve: to control compressed air flow into the tank.

System shut off valve includes control valve: to separate oil system from oil supply in case of revision, mtce

Manual shut off, terminal board, level: drain valve, level indicator, manual valves: switches for control of air refill and oil pumps.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل.

  • The maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, gloves, ... etc.

  • The unit must be out of service.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete tool set includes manual wrenches ... etc.

  • Cleaning rags.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Clean the pressure vessel.
