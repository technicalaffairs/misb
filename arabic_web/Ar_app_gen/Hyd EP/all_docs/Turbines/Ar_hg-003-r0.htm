مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection (P1), which must be performed every 6 months..

The technical specification of the hydraulic Gov. S/N 6560/61/62 - L2170mm, W = 920 mm, H=2200mm, Q=45/56L/min power = 15KW, tank vol =800L

The first draft initiated & tested by eng. Abd El Mohsen Mohammed general manager of  Nag Hammadi Hyd. PS,  and certified by by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem HPGC  inspection and quality control general director.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintanance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل 

  • Wear the appropriate safety and healthy equipment as safety shoes, helmet, breathing mask

    • ... etc

  • Stop pumps and switch off the power supply

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet and safety shoes

  • Set of wrenches, Cleaning material

  • Screw driver, grease for lubricating

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Test the control and safety instrument

  2. Check up the noise at operation

  3. التحقق من محاذاة المضخات

  4. فحص المرشحات و تغييره إذا لزم الأمر

  5. Check up the pump current
