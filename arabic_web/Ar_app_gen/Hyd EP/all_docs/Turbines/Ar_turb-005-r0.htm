مقدمة

This document outline the equipment inspection checklist I1, which must be performed on monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the title block .

Turbine bearing is located up to the hub and is the first bearing from the bottom of the unit under the thrust bearing

This bearing function is to carry the radial water force component and to keep the shaft vertically  

The first draft is initiated and tested by eng. Ahmed Abd El Kader Mahmoud, accepted by eng. Atty Seiffen general manager, approved by eng. Mohamed Abdel-Baset  head sector of Aswan Dam (1&2) power stations and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem HPGC  inspection and quality control general director.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Maintenance staff should be will trained and qualified or directly supervised by some one who is.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • يجب أن تتوقف الوحدة.

  • The oil circulation pumps must be off.

  • Gate lock and break on.

Tools and Equipment

  • Safety and healthy equipment

  • Wrenches

Work to be Carried Out

  1. Check the tightening of all outer visual parts of the bearing

  2. Change the coupling rubber if it needed

  3. Clean the strainer of the oil

  4. Clean the heat exchanger by reversing the pass of the water circulation
