مقدمة

This document outlines the testing procedure P1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

The present electric - hydraulic governor system represents the latest development in a continuing program - designed to provide the best equipment available for hydraulic turbine speed control - the hydraulic turbine governor is no longer just a speed response control of the turbine

The first draft initiated eng. Khaled Saeed Mohamed, , tested by eng. Hamdy El-Abd, accepted by eng. Nagy Nageeb Saleh: general director, approved by eng. Mohamed Abd Elbasat head sector of Aswan Dam Hydraulic power station and certified by eng. Kamel Raghib Hakem: HEPC quality control general director and MPIS coordinator.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Maintenance staff should be qualified trained or directly supervised by some one who is

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear the personal safety and healthy protective equipment as safety shoes, helmet ... etc

  • Switch off the DC power supply before inserting or withdrawing the relay.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Signal generator variable from 0-5000 Hz, and minimum from 6 to 8 Volt (RMS)

  • Electric frequency counter to accurately set the signal generator output to the desired frequency

  • Multi-range DC voltmeter

  • A good oscilloscope and digital voltmeter are very handly to have, although not always necessary

العمل المراد تنفيذه

تحضير

  1. Turn off the governor power supply

  2. Pull all modules in all racks

  3. Turn on the governor power supply transfer switch to the test position

  4. Check the main supply (AC-DC) 

Calibration Procedure

  1. Set the gates to 0 % and record the ammeter reading on the terminal (42 or 43) in 723 digital control, it is must be read (4 m amp)
    If the above value has not a (4 m amp) we can adjust it from (zero) pot. position isolation amplifier

  2. Set  the gates to 100 % and record the ammeter reading on the terminal (42 or 43) in 723 digital control, it is must be read (20 m amp)
    If the above value has not a (20 m amp) we can adjust it from (span) pot. position isolation amplifier
