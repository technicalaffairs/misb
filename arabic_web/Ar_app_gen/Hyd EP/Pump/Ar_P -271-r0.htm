السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل و يجب أن يحمل موظفو الصيانة المؤهلون فئة السلامة المناسبة

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear the safety and healthy protective equipment as safety shoes, helmet .... etc

  • Stop pump and switch off the power supply

  • Close the out let and inlet valves

  • Put suitable warning boards

أدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet and safety shoes

  • Set of wrench

  • Alignment equipment

  • Grease for lubricating

  • Cleaning material

العمل ليكون نفذت 

  1. Check the packing of the gland of the pump ads or change

  2. Check the packing of the gland of the outlet and inlet valves

  3. Check the bolts of the pump and motor

  4. Check the alignment of the pump

  5. Operate the pump and check the noise and bearing

  6. Check the current
