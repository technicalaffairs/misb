 Dewatering pump

إجراء:
Equipment Inspection (I1-Y1)

Date Approved:      03 Feb 2009 

تاريخ المراد مراجعتها:     Feb 2014

مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed  annually  bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The pump are centrifugal horizontal with redial impeller and spiral casing provided with feet axial suction orifice and delivery orifice in an upward position

The pump is assembled to an electric asynchronous three phase motor by means of a flexible coupling

The unite block pump motor is assembled on an electro - welvedsteel daseplate

V : 380V
P : 55KW
N : 1450rpm
H : 35m

The first draft initiated and tested by eng. Ahmad Abd-Elrady Mohamed , accepted by eng. Abd-Elkarem Mosa Ahmad , approved by eng. Yosef Mahmod Ali general manager of Esna power station and certified by eng. Mohamed Osama Soliman: HPGC quality general director & MPIS coordinator

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety ID category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear the safety and healthy protective equipment as safety shoes, helmet .... etc

  • The pump is out of service

  • تم فصل المحرك من مركز عملائي ولوحة التحكم

أدوات والمعدات

  • Mechanical tools (complete set of manual wrench - brushes - etc))

  • Blanco 1 ton

  • بعض قطع الغيار الخاصة (الختم الميكانيكي - محمل كروي - إلخ)

  • Tighten hexagonal screws with atorqe wrench

العمل ليكون نفذت 

  1. Check that the shaft can freely rotate by hand

  2. Check the tightening of the suction and delivery flanges

  3. Check that ball bearings are filled up with grease

  4. Check the mechanical seal

  5. Check that for the presence of foreign substances which could damage the pump
