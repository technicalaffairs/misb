مقدمة

This document lists the equipment inspection I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

A group of relays operate when the receives a signal from the protection system to initiate the five fighting system and starting it. These relays con also operate when we push the manual push button. When these relays operate they start the five fighting pump and opening the electric five valves. The fed from automatic 5at 220Vdc

المسودة الأولى بدأها المهندس. محمد شعبان، tested by eng. حسن أحمد, accepted by general director eng. Salem Ahmed Aly, وافق عليها المهندس. عبد الكريم أبو زيد إسماعيل: رئيس قطاع هيدروليك السد العالي محطة توليد الكهرباء، ومعتمدة من قبل المهندس. كامل راغب حكيم: مدير عام مراقبة الجودة بشركة HPGC، ومنسق MPIS.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes, belts, work gloves

  • Switch off the automatics DC Power supply (5AT).

  • الصمامات اليدوية لإطلاق النار على مقربة

أدوات والمعدات

  • Personal safety equipment, safety goggles, and rubber gloves and rubber apron.

  • Potable air blower.

  • Set of common/screw drivers and cleaning material (rag/cloth).

  • Digital multimeter.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Clean contacts for relays 51pn, 52pn, 53pn, 54pn, 52pb, 55pb.

  2. Clean contacts for generator valves (CB).

  3. Check fire fighting control circuit.
