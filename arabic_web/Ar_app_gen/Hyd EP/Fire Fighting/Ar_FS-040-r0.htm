مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل.

  • The maintenance staff should carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • ينبغي أن يكون موظفي الصيانة مدربين تدريبا جيدا أو مؤهلين أو يشرف عليهم مباشرة شخص ما

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear the personal protective equipment must used as safety shoes, safety helmet ...etc

الأدوات والمعدات

  • الأميتر الرقمي

  • Screw Drivers

  • Jumpers

  • dry spray

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. أعد ربط المحطات في حجرة التحكم

  2. Clean the contacts of the main contactors using dry spray.

Control Function Check

  1. Connect the AC supply (380 Vac) to control cubicle of the electrical valve.

  2. Choose the mechanical valve of water by hand.

  3. Simulate the protection signals for transformer differential protection and bucholz protection cosequantly and observe the operation of electrical valve of water.

  4. After each protection signal, close the electric valve by push button (close), on control cubicle.

  5. Open the water mechanical valve by hand.

  6. Open the mechanicl valve by push button (open & stop) together.

  7. Close the mechanical valve by push button (close).

  8. Observe the alarm signal at local control board of the unit.
