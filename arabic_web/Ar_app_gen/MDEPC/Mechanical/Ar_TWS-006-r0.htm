مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be carried out every nine month as indicated by M9 in the above title block.               

This equipment used for clean intake water from any deposits suspended.

The first draft initiated by eng. Shady Ghazy, tested by maintenance manager eng. Ahmad Shana , revised by maintenance general manager: eng. Omran Abd El Hamid Shoaib,  revised by MDEPC quality control eng. Abouda El Bana and approved by eng. Ahmed Sawan, Nubria power station head sector

This document certified by eng. Samir Abd Algalil inspection & quality control general director, MPIS coordinator and approved by eng. Mohamed Nagieb Inspection and quality control head sector

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should be carry the Suitable safety category,

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes, earplug and gloves pairs.

  • Isolate the screen mechanically and electrically.  

أدوات والمعدات

  • Wires to raise up the screen cover
