السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل.

  • The maintenance staff should be trained, qualified, and carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Personal protective equipment must be used as safety shoes, and safety helmet.

  • Shutdown the pump.

  • افصل الطاقة توريد ورف خارج قاطع الدائرة.

  • وضع علامة حمراء على مضخة.

  • Close the shutoff valves in the (suction & discharge& balancing &min .flow ).

  • Let the pumps cool down to about < 80oC measured at the branches, then seal off the cooling water pipes and close all other supply lines.

  • Loosen all auxiliary lines, such as circulation, cooling liquid, lube oil.

  • The safety department must check the isolation and surrounding the zone of work.

 

 

 

 

الأدوات و المعدات

  • مجموعة كاملة من أدوات.

  • Crane.

  • Alignment indicators, filler.

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

  1. Check oil level and viscosity.

  2.  Check heat.

  3. Check noise.

  4. Check vibration.

  5. Check foundation.

  6. تحقق من التعبئة.

  7. Check leakage of water.

  8. Check looses of bolts.

Dismantling

 Disconnect the motor electrically.

  • Isolate the pump ( Close the isolating valves ) and drain it as completely as possible.

  • افصل الأنابيب المساعدة (تبريد المياه).

  • Uncoupling pump from motor and disconnect any safety devices as well as pressure and temperature measuring instrumentation.  

  • Disconnect pump from suction and discharge piping.

  • Remove coupling spacer

  • Drain the oil.

  • Remove the casing adapter frame nuts.

  • Pull out the complete bearing housing with rotor. 

  • Carry the complete bearing housing with rotor eventually to the workshop to continue dismantling.

    • إجراءات

    1. Change the gaskets if necessary.

    2. Change the packing if necessary

    3. Change the oil if necessary.

    4. All parts as bearing must be checked prior to assembly and repaired or replaced if necessary .

    ال steps of assembling are the reverse steps of dismantling
