مقدمة

يوضح هذا المستند فحص المعدات I1، الذي يجب إجراؤه عليه القواعد الأسبوعية كما هو موضح بواسطة W1 في كتلة العنوان.

This 90 Watt Power Supply Module is a rackmounted unit that plugs directly into a 48pin backplanemounted connector. It provides +5volt, +12 volt and -12 volt power, and logic level sequencing signals to the backplane.

The first draft is initiated by eng. Mohamed Tawfeeq, tested by eng. Bahaa Abd El-Wahab, revised by MDEPC quality control eng. Adel El Naggar, accepted by general manager eng. Mohamed Elshreif, approved by eng. Ahmed Sawan head sector of Nubaria power station .

This document certified by eng. Samir Abd El Gillil MDEPC quality control general manager and MPIS coordinator and approved by eng. Mohammed Nageb quality control head sector.

Features

  • Operation from 48 VDC.

  • Three output voltages, 90 watts total,

  • +5 VDC output up to 18 amps,

  • +12 VDC output up to 1.5 amps,

  • -12 VDC output up to 1 amp,

  • Slidein rack mount construction.

  • Electronic short circuit over current protection provided on 5 volt bus.

  • Two rack operation from a single power supply
