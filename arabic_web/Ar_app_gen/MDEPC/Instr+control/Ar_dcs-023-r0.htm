 AB 121 RTD Inpurt Module
Alstom

رقم الوثيقة:   DCS-023-r0

صدرت إلى:       النوبارية PS

حالة:          موافقة

إجراء:
فحص المعدات (I1-W1)

موافقة Date:      24 Nov 2008

تاريخ المراد مراجعتها:     Nov 2013

 

مقدمة

يوضح هذا المستند فحص المعدات I1، الذي يجب إجراؤه عليه القواعد الأسبوعية كما هو موضح بواسطة W1 في كتلة العنوان.

The AB121 is an analogue acquisition module used to acquire 8 low level measurements from 100 W/0C˚ (3wire) platinum resistance temperature detectors (RTDs) It isolates and pre-processes the signals for the CE 2000

The first draft is initiated by eng. Mohamed Tawfeeq, tested by eng. Bahaa Abd El-Wahab, revised by MDEPC quality control eng. Adel El Nagga, accepted by general manager eng. Mohamed Elshreif, approved by eng. Ahmed Sawan head sector of Nubaria power station .

This document certified by eng. Samir Abd El Gillil MDEPC quality control general manager and MPIS coordinator and approved by eng. Mohammed Nageb quality control head sector.

Features

Dimension:

  • Height: 233 mm

  • Depth: 220 mm

  • Width: 1 slot = 25.4 mm

  • Weight: 0.400 kg

عملية

The AB121 module includes:

  • An analogue interface consisting of:

  • A switching converter

  • ثمانية مدخلات منخفضة المستوى (RTC0 إلى RTD7)، كل منها مزود بـ شبكة ترشيح وتنقل وثمانية مصادر طاقة RTD

  • A 0 V test channel and a full-scale test channel

  • A channel reserved for calibration

  • A static multiplexing circuit

  • A measuring chain (voltage/frequency converter, and amplifier)

  • An intelligent unit consisting of a micro-controller with the following:

  • EPROM/RAM/EEPROM

  • A clock with a maximum frequency of 16 MHZ

  • ثلاثة عدادات مستقلة 16 بت،

  • An analogue interface control circuit,

  • An interface with the 2000 I/O backplane bus, consisting of:

  • A circuit for plugging in with power on,

  • واجهة تابعة لحافلة الإدخال/الإخراج لعام 2000،

The AB120 performs the measurement acquisition, in the course of which it:  

  • Scans the inputs cyclically,

  • Dإلخ الخطوط المفتوحة (فصل أجهزة الاستشعار)،

  • Corrects the offset or gain drift errors,

  • Linearizes the measurements

  • Converts the measurements into physical quantities (C)

The AB 121 module

  • Tتقديرات دوائرها الخاصة،

  • Converses with the higher level via the 2000 I/O bus,

 

Controls and indications on the front panel

A green LED on the front panel indicates that the module is working. It goes out in the following cases:

  • لا يمكن لوحدة PU الخاصة بالحامل CE2000 الوصول إلى الوحدة،

  • The module has detected a hardware fault, 

  • The module has detected a user fault: loss of the process voltage, disconnection of a channel.

حماية

General protection on the process voltage +Vp by CTP with maximum tripping current at 20C: 0.45 A.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued

  • Maintenance staff should be well trained and qualified.

  • Caution marks must be hanged on the work place, safety rope around the work place.

أدوات والمعدات

  • Complete tool set.  

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

  1. Check the supply voltage

  2. Check the interface leds
