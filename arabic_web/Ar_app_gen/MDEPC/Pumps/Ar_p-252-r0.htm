This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed on yearly bases as indicated by Y1 in the above title block.

This pump is used to Sodium Hypochlorite unloading from the sodium hypochlorite car tank to the storage tank

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask ...etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor

  • Isolate the pump mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves.

  • Be sure pump must be completely drained.

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

Pump in operation

  1. Check pump capacity, pressure and power .

  2. Check the Noisy of the Pump.

  3. Check pump leak

  4. Measure vibration

Pump out of operation

  1. Check the fixation

  2. Adjust mechanical seal

  3. Adjust alignment

Starting the Pump

Before starting the pump, the following steps should be checked

  1. Check the direction of rotation of motor before starting it is clockwise

  2. The on-off valve in the suction from the tank, must be completely open, while the air discharge valve after complete priming - if applicable - and the draining valve must be closed

  3. The control valve in delivery to the pump, must be partially open

 

Attendance and checking during operation of the pump, the following should be checked:

  1. Check of flow rate delivery head and absorbed power

  2. Follow the indication of the flow - motor and manometer installed

  3. Check the water leakage of pump

  4. يفحص ال pump pressure
