السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask ...etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor

  • Isolate the pump mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves.

  • Be sure pump must be completely drained.

الأدوات و المعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • سائل تنظيف، سحب يدوي

  • Feeler gauge, dial indicator

  • Vibrometer, straight edge, rags

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

المضخة قيد التشغيل

  1. Check pump capacity, pressure and power if pump performance does satisfy your process requirements and process requirements have not changed pump should be disassembled, inspection and worn parts should be replaced otherwise a system inspection should be done

  2. تحقق من noise التابع pump.

  3. Measure vibration level for bearings and compare it with the setting value then analyze it and determine what to do

  4. Check the temperature of bearing

  5. Check the leakage of the mechanical seal

Pump out of operation

  1. Check alignment and adjust if needed

  2. Check the fixation

Starting the Pump

Before starting the pump, the following steps should be checked

  1. Turn rotation element by hand to make sure it rotates freely

  2. Check the direction of rotation of motor before starting it is clockwise

  3. Fill the pump casing with the liquid

  4. Turn rotation element by hand to make sure it rotates freely

  5. Check suction and discharge piping for leaks and make sure for flange bolts are securely tightened

  6. Install coupling guard on flexible coupled unit

الحضور والفحص أثناء تشغيل المضخة ويجب ما يلي تم التحقق:

  1. Check of flow rate و تسليم رئيس

  2. Check the shaft seal for leaks

  3. Check that the current absorbed by the motor dose not exceed the rated value shown on the name plate

  4. يفحص ال pump pressure

  5. Fully open gate valve (if any) in suction line and close gate valve in discharge line

  6. Fill suction line with liquid and completely prime pump

  7. ابدأ المحرك (المضخة) قم على الفور بإجراء فحص بصري للمضخة وأنابيب الشفط تسربات الضغط

  8. Immediately after pump reaches full operating speed slowly open discharge gate valve until complete system flow is achieved

  9. Check discharge piping for leaks
