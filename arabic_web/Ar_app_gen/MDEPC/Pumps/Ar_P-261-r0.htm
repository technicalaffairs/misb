مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection list I1, which must be performed every one year as indicated by (Y1) in the above title block.

This pump is used to suction the sludge wasting water from the waste clarifiers and discharged it in the sludge thickener.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل و يجب أن يحمل موظفو الصيانة المؤهلون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير مطلوب المحيطة بمنطقة العمل.

  • ارتداء معدات السلامة والصحة كخوذة الأمان، وأحذية السلامة، وقناع التنفس ...إلخ.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the pump motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of the pump motor

  • Isolate the pump mechanically by closing delivery and suction valves

  • Be sure pump must be completely drainage.

أدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة.

  • Cleaning fluid. Manual puller.

  • وجع الفرقة لتجميع الجزء الثابت

  • Chain wrench for stator assembly

  • Stator clamp for stator assembly

  • Stator assembly device

  • Set hexagon socket screw

  • Set pin extractor

  • وجع عزم الدوران

  • Caliper gauge with depth scale

  • كماشة التجميع للحلقة الدائرية

  • Filling device for gear joints

  • Packing screw

العمل ليكون نفذت 

Pump in operation

  1. Check pump capacity, pressure and power .   

  2. Check the Noisy of the Pump.

  3. Measure vibration level for bearings and compare it with the setting value, then analyze it and determine what to do

  4. Check the Temperature of bearing

  5. Check the leakage  of the mechanical seal 

 

Pump خارج الخدمة

  1. تحقق من المحاذاة وقارنها بقيمة الإعداد

  2. Adjust the mechanical seal  leakage

  3. Adjust piping for leaks

  4. Check the fixation

Starting the Pump

Before starting the pump, the following steps should be checked

  1. Check the direction of rotation of motor before starting; it is clockwise

  2. The check must be carried out be qualified and competent  operators , starting the motor for a few second

  3. Open the valves and vents and close drain .

  4. filling the pump casing with the medium , in case of high viscosity media fill with the liquid .Pump priming is vital to ensure lubrication of the rubber stator.

Attendance and checking during operation of the pump, the following should be checked:

  1. Check discharge, pressure and power

  2. Check the shaft seal for leaks

  3. Check heating of pump .

  4. تحقق صاخبة .

  5. Check foundation .
