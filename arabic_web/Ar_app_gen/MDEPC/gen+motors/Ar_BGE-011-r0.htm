مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1 for Mitsubishi frame 2 Steam Turbine Generator carbon brushes, which must be performed on weekly bases as indicated by W1 in the title block.

Technical Specifications:

Quantity  

80 brush (40 brush at +ve & 40 brush ve)

Manufacture

Mitsubishi

Brush current density

6 ~ 8 A/cm

Material

Natural black-lead brush NCC-634

Manufacture

U.C.C , USA      

Effective wearing allowance for brush

60 mm            

The first draft initiated by eng. Eman El Henawey, tested by eng. Wageh Khalil, revised by MDEPC quality control eng. Adel El-Naggar, accepted by general manager eng. Ali Zahran and approved by eng. Ahmed Sawwan head sector of Nubaria Power Station.

This document certified by eng. Samir Abd El Gillil MDEPC quality control general manager and MPIS coordinator and approved by eng. Mohammed Nageb quality control head sector.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued.

  • Personal protective equipment must be used (safety shoes, Helmet, breathing mask, safety glasses).

  • Maintenance staff should be well trained and qualified.

  • Caution marks must be hanged on the work place, safety fence around the work place.

أدوات والمعدات

  • Brushes  measuring tool.

  • Blower.

  • Screw driver.

 

 

العمل ليكون نفذت 

  1. Check the brush wearing conditions

  2. Check brush motion .

  3. Check brush chattering .

  4. Overheat of pigtail.

  5. Check Brush's slide surface conditions.

  6. Clean the slip ring surface.
