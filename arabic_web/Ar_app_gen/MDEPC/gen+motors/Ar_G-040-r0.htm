احتياطات السلامة

  • A wأورك permit must be issued.

  • The maintenance staff should be trained, qualified, and carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask, safety glasses ... etc.

  • Generator must be shut downed and isolated

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask, safety glasses ... etc.

  • منفاخ، Brushes.

  • Cيميل fluids as Freon - TF, Chlorothene.

  • Megger, Digital multi-meters.

  • Brushes measuring tools.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Primary visual inspections.

  2. Clean all surfaces using brushes and air dry compressor. 

  3. Clean the all surfaces by using cleaning fluids such as Freon - TF, chlorothene. 

  4. Paint the all surfaces of the core if it needed.  

  5. Inspect the ends of the stator and rotor winding for deposits of oil and evidence of corona discharge

  6. Inspect the winding and insulation core fully.  

  7. Measure stator & rotor insulation resistance. see figure 1, 2

  8. Check LV & DC distribution panels.

  9. Check SFC/SEE cubicles, cleaning, check all alarms للوظائف الصحيحة، توصيلات الكابلات والثايرستور وبطاقات التحكم.

  10. Check auxiliary transformers.

  11. تحقق من مقاومة عزل المحمل.

  12. Check slip rings for exciter current transmission on contamination and damage. Finish turn or grind out of true or non faced slip rings.

  13. Check grounding shaft brushes.

  14. Inspect make up air filter for contamination.

  15. Check generator slip ring brushes.

  16. Check neutral point thermometer temperature.

  17. Check operating air coolers on proper ventilation.

  18. التحقق من حالة حلقات الانزلاق.

  19. Check brush holders.

  20. فحص أنابيب مدخل ومخرج مياه التبريد.

  21. Check condition of temperature and pressure.

      Required Insulation Resistance of Stator Winding

    تحدد المعايير الدولية الجديدة العزل مقاومة لا تقل عن 1 ميجا أوم لكل كيلو فولت من جهد المولد عند 40 oج(104 oF)

    A correction of the insulation resistance measured at the winding temperature prevailing during the measurement to the above reference temperature can be made using the diagram in figure 2.

    If the insulation resistance measured is lower than the specified minimum value, the neutral connections must be separated and the insulation resistance of the individual winding phases measured.

    During the test, the other two phases which are not involved should be grounded. If the insulation resistance of the individual phases are likewise lower than the required limit value, drying of the windings will be indispensable.

    Great differences in the insulation resistance values are indicative of local contaminations, e.g. insulators in bus duct or terminal bushings. If predicable additional connections should be separated.

    If the measure does not result in an improvement, it is recommended to obtain the services of a specialist from the manufacture's works.

     Measurement of Insulation Resistance

    Prior to each measurement, the generator must be de-excited and any static charges removed by grounding the windings. The windings should be grounded after each measurement for the duration of the recharging period.

    The insulation resistance of the stator is measured between the winding copper and the stator core.

     Required Insulation Resistance of Rotor Winding

    The insulation resistance should amount to > 1 ميجا أوم عند 40 oج(104 oF)

    At winding temperature other than 40 oC, correction can be made using the curve shown in Figure 2.

     Measurement of Insulation Resistance

    The insulation resistance should be measured between one slipring and the generator shaft, using a insulation resistance meter with a voltage < 500V. This voltage will present no danger to any of the devices in the excitation circuit.

    The duration of the test should be approximately two minutes.

    After each measurement, the winding capacitance should be discharged for not less than two minutes.

    The insulation resistance of the entire excitation circuit should be measured with the brushes applied. If an insufficient insulation resistance value is obtained. all slipring carbon brushes should be lifted off and separate measurement conducted on the rotor winding and excitation circuit.
