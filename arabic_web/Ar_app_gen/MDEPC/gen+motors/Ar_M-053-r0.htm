مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection P1, which must be performed every two years as indicated by Y5 in the title block.                                                                         

This document initiated by eng. Amany Elmetwaly, tested by eng. Hoda Ali, revised by quality control eng. Adel Abdelkader Elnagar at MDPE , accepted by general director eng. متولي  محمود الهنداوي , approved by eng. Farouk Awad head sector of  Talkha P.S.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area.

  • ارتداء خوذة السلامة، والأحذية، أزواج سدادة الأذن والقفازات.

  • The motor must be out of service.

  • Circuit breaker in test position.

أدوات والمعدات

  • Suitable tool set, Air Blower, Megger device

  • Greasing machine, Multimeter.

  • قماش ناعم ل أغراض التنظيف

  • Brushes, (soft brushes)

العمل الذي يتعين القيام به

  1. Measure and record the insulation of motor winding, repair if required

  2. Clean the body of the motor and ventilation holes using air blower and brushes

  3. Remove the motor bearing bracket.

  4. Remove the rotor, avoid touch the stator end winding by the rotor.

  5. Check the ball bearings, grease / lubricate or replace if needed. (bearings and grease must be kept free of dirt).

  6. Clean motor winding using soft brushes

  7. أعد تجميع المحرك.

  8. Check all the tightens of the power and control circuits connections.

  9. قياس وتسجيل مقاومة العاصمة لف المحرك.

  10. Measure and record the insulation of motor winding after maintenance and befor running the motor.

  11. Connect the power source to the motor.

  12. Run the motor and check the ball bearing or roller bearing housing temperature not more than 80C .

  13. Check lubrication and alignment if temperature exceed this limit

  14. Check vibration or noises, tighten the bolts if required

  15. تحقق من تشغيل المحرك من التشغيل المحلي والبعيد في موضع الاختبار

  16. Check all protection of the motor by protection group (O.C, S.C., U.V)
