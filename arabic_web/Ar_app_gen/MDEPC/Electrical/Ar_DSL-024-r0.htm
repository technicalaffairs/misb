مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on weekly bases as indicated by W1 in the title block.

The first draft initiated and tested by eng. Eman El-Henawey, revised by eng. Wageh Khalil, reviewed by MDEPCo. quality control eng. Abel El Naggar, accepted by eng. Ali Zahran general manager, approved by eng. Ahmed Sawan head sector of Nubaria power station and certified by eng. Mohamed Nageb Inspection and quality control head sector.

Technical Specifications:

Batteries
Number and connection 6:3 parallel of 2 series
Direct current voltage 24 V
سعة 200 AH
Type Lead - acid

Battery charger
Manufacture Electra molins S.A
Type CAB - 24/40
سعة 40 A
Supply rated voltage 3 ~ 400 V, 50 Hz
Output voltage 24 Vdc

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued

  • Personal protective equipment must be used (safety shoes, helmet breathing mask, safety glasses)

  • Maintenance staff should be well trained and qualified

Tools and Equipments

  • Tester (voltmeter, ammeter).

  • Cleaning cloths.

  • Cleaning brushes tool .

Work to be carried out

  1. Check the ventilation system .

  2. Check batteries current .

  3. Measure room temperature .

  4. Visual inspection for cables bolts and joint .

  5. Check battery charger indication lamps .

  6. Check battery charger input and output voltage .

  7. Check connection and disconnection in case of fault in power supply .

  8. Check battery charger alarms .
