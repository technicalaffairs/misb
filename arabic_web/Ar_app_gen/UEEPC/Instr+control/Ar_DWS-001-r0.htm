 Drainage Water System

إجراء:
فحص المعدات (I1-Y1)

مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be carried out on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

The  first draft initiated by eng. Zakaria Mohamed Abd El Rehim, tested by eng. Zakaria Mohamed Abd El Rehim accepted by general director eng. Osama Ahmed Hasaneen revised by eng. Mohammed Osama Solima  HPGC general director quality control and approved by eng. Khalaf Allah Mahmoud Abd El gaber head sector of Aswan Hydraulic power station

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار أمر العمل

  • The maintenance staff should be qualified and carry the suitable ID safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes breathing mask, safety glass ... etc

أدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet, shoes breathing mask safety glass ... etc

  • Safety fence caution marks

  • Digital Ammeter

  • Screw driver

العمل ليكون نفذت 

  1. Clean control cubicle by vacuum cleaner

  2. Retighten terminals in control cubicles and connection box

  3. مزود الطاقة:-

  • Check the input/output voltage to

  • DC/DC converter (TC1)
    I/P 220 VDC & O/P 24 VDC

  • DC/AC inverter (TC1)
    I/P 220 VDC & O/P voltage 24 VAC

Alarm system

  1. Switch off MCE (FA) for alarm system

  2. Racked out all electronic cards and clean it by vacuum cleaner and brush

  3. Heck jumpers on each card

  4. Racked in all cards

  5. Switch on MCB (FA) and make function test for alarm system

  6. Check the indication lamps and change the burned lamps

  7. Check the electrical coupling between two sumps by key switch position to(sump1+ sump2)

Level switches in the sump

  1. Check the level switches and clean it by rags and water

  2. Check the function for each level switch by increase the water level in the sump

  3. Observe the signal level in the control room in the event and alarm list

  4. The water levels in the sump are-

  • Water level too high (     ) alarm

  • Water level high (     ) alarm

  • مضخة التصريف 2 على إنذار (    ).

  • Drain pump1 on (      ) event

  • Main pump on (      ) event

  • Drain pump 1+2 off (      ) event

  • Drain pump 1+2 blocked (      ) event

Alarm detectors
Leakage in the stator housing

  1. Check mA in the device circuit and check the value of resistance

  2. The normal value of mA is (7 mA) and resistance is (1.5 k ohm)

  3. The alarm values are (30 mA) & (330 Ohm)

تحمل كاشف درجات الحرارة

  1. The normal resistance at temperature 20c is (108 Ohm).

  2. Record results in the maintenance file.
