مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1 which must be performed every year as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The technical specification of the unit generator:

يكتب: SAV 750 /240 / 60 THY
Voltage: 11 KV
Current: 2600 أ
تصنيف: 49500 KVA
يف: 0.95
RPM: 100 دورة في الدقيقة

The first draft was initiated by eng. Hussein Hashem Mohamed, tested by eng. Naser El Sayed Abd EL-Aziz, accepted by general director eng. Mohamed Osama Soliman Selim, approved by eng. Mohamed Abdel-Baset  head sector of Aswan Dam (1&2) power stations and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem HPGC MPIS coordinatori and inspection & quality control general director.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح عمل، والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear your safety equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask, safety glass ... etc

  • Put the generator out of service

  • The generator circuit breaker is switched off

  • تم إيقاف تشغيل قاطع مجال المولد

  • The bus duct equipment are earthed

  • The brake is applied manually for the rotating parts of the generator

  • The CO2 extinguishers system is isolated

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Complete kit of tools

  • Measuring instrument (Ampero - meter - magger)

  • Dry rags

  • Alcholl

  • Compressed air

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Measure the brush length and change brushes which has low length

  2. Check brush pressure and replace weak springs

  3. Check brush holder position and correct

  4. Clean the brush holder and brush using alchool

  5. Clean the slip ring

  6. Check generator air cooler and cleaning by removing upper and lower cover

  7. Check generator space heater

  8. Check stator and rotor winding and cleaning

  9. Check grounding brush and cleaning, measuring

  10. Measure the insulation resistance of rotor and stator winding

  11. تنظيف قناة الحافلة المعدات

  12. Clean the netural point pannel

  13. Check all signal protection and monitoring equipment

  14. Check all accessible bolts and nuts
