مقدمة

This document outlines the maintenance procedures P3, which must be performed every two years as indicated by Y2 in the title block

The first draft initiated by eng. Ahmed Fawzy Badry, tested by eng. Nabeel saad Ibrahem at 01 Jan 2004, accepted by general director eng. Shawqy Mahmoud Attala, and approved by eng. Mohamed Mahdey Rezq Alnshar Head Sector of Sidi Kirir 1&2 Power Station.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category,

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

    •

  • Wear safety helmet, and safety shoes.

  • The breaker of the motor must be isolated

  • The motor windings and capacitors must be discharged used a solid wire first before touching the windings

  • The motor needs to be disassembled for this maintenance procedure to be carried out

أدوات والمعدات

  • Dry cloth, Dry air from air blower or surface line

  • New filters if necessary

  • Megger for insulation measuring

  • Electrical equipment kit

  • Volatile electrical cleaning fluid

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Carryout the MPIS document No. M-003-r0 every six months

  2. Clean the rotor air path with dry air.

  3. Wash the winding with volatile materials.

  4. Measure the motor insulation resistance.
