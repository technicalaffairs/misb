Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety ID category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Safety and healthy equipment must be used as safety shoes, and safety Helmet ... etc.

  • Isolation and proper earthing is required.

  • Before starting any work on the machine, check that it is switched off and blocked up for switching ON again.

Tools and Equipment

  • Safety and healthy equipment.

  • Tool set for tightness, cleaning tools as cloth.... etc

  • Press-on and detaching device, AVO meter

  • Air blower.

    • Work to be Carried out

    1. Inspect the electrical cubical and clean by air blower: air pressure less than 3bar.

    2. Check the electric motors.

    3. Clean the hydrogen unit

    4. Check the electric cubical contactors, timers, and change any bad components  

    5. Check the motors, and fan, and change the bearings

    6. Inspect the hydrogen unit, check the carbon branches, change it if needed, and change the bad coils
