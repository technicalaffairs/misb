This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, which must be performed on a weekly bases as indicated by W1 in the title block .

The exciter data   2.42 MVA,  525 Vac, Y connection, 2420 A , PF=0.5 , 50HZ, 3000 RPM

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipments as safety healthy, safety shoes, gloves pairs, breathing mask, safety glass etc .

  • Take care that the unit at service.
