مقدمة

This document lists the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Mohamed Salah El Din Badawy, tested by eng. Hassan Mohamed Amer, accepted by eng. Ahmed El Hosiny general director, revised by EDEPC inspection & quality control eng. Adel Abdel Qader EL Nagar, and approved by eng.  Mohamed El Sarag Damietta PS head sector

This document is accepted by eng. Samir Abd El Gilil EDEPC MPIS coordinator, and certified by accountant; Mahmoud Mohamed Soliman; EDEPC admin and financial inspection, quality control General Director and supervisor of inspection and quality control قطاع الرأس.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار تصريح عمل وصيانة مؤهلة يحمل الموظفون فئة معرف السلامة المناسبة.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

    •

  • Wear standard personal safety and healthy equipment as safety shoes, safety helmet, breathing mask, ... etc.

  • The unit must be out service.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Megger (500 5000) volt.

  • Multimeter, Air blower, Cleaning solvent 

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • Contact resistance tester.
