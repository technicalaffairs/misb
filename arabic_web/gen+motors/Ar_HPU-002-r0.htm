مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Eid Lotfy Tolba, tested by eng. Ebrahim Youssef Abd El Latif, revised by EDEPC quality control eng Adel Abdel Qader El Nagar and eng. Saad Mahmoud Bassuny, accepted by general director eng. Sayed Mahmoud Hessain Farag and approved by eng. Mokhtar Abd El-Latif Mady head sector of Ataka PS.

تم قبول هذه الوثيقة من قبل المهندس. سمير عبد الجليل؛ منسق EDEPC MPIS ومعتمد بقلم المحاسب محمود سليمان مدير عام EDEPC التفتيش المالي ومشرف رئيس التفتيش ومراقبة الجودة القطاع.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety ID category

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير تحيط بالمكان منطقة العمل مطلوبة

  • Safety and healthy equipment must be used as safety shoes, and safety Helmet ... etc.

  • Isolation and proper earthing is required

  • Before starting any work on the machine, check that it is switched off and blocked up for switching on again

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety and healthy equipment.

  • Tool set for tightness, cleaning tools as cloth.... etc

  • Press-on and detaching device.

  • Air blower.

    • العمل المراد تنفيذه

    1. Inspect the electrical cubical and clean by air blower: air pressure less than 3 bar.

    2. Check the electric motors.

    3. Clean the hydrogen unit

    4. Check the electric cubical contactors, timers.

    5. Check the motors, and fan.

    6. Inspect the hydrogen unit transformer, check the carbon brushes, and change it if needed .
