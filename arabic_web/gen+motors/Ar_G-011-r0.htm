Minor Maintenance Procedures (P1-Y5)

موافقة Date:   09 Feb 2005

تاريخ المراد مراجعتها:  Feb 2010

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

مقدمة

This document outlines the minor maintenance procedures P1, which must be performed every 5 years as indicated by Y5 in the title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Mohamed Salah El Din Badawy, tested by eng Abd El Wahab Abd El Wahab Ahmed, accepted by eng. Ahmed El Hosiny general director, revised by EDEPC inspection & quality control eng. Adel Abdel Qader EL Nagar, and approved by eng.  Mohamed El Sarag Damietta PS head sector

This document is accepted by eng. Samir Abd El Gilil EDEPC MPIS coordinator, and certified by accountant; Mahmoud Mohamed Soliman; EDEPC admin and financial inspection, quality control General Director and supervisor of inspection and quality control قطاع الرأس.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Maintenance staff should be well trained, qualified or directly supervised by some one who is.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

    •

  • Personal protective equipment must be used (safety shoes, helmet, breathing mask, safety glasses).

  • The operator, who is inside the stator, is deprived from any metallic or magnetic object (kees, phones, tape measure, chain ).

  • Protect the inferior part of endwinding and core, with a rubber carpet or plastic film, which will move with a make progress expertise.

  • Supply of necessity, by intermediate of separate cubicle, the eventual appliance, and lighting put on work inside the core stator.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Megger (500 5000) volt

  • Multimeter, Air blower

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة

  • Contact resistance tester

  • Cleaning solvent
