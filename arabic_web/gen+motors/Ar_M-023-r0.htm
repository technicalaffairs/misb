مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every three months as indicated by M3 in the title block. This document used for the boiler feed pump motor of unit 1.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Before starting any work on the machine, check that it is switched off and blocked up for switching on again.

  • The unit out of service.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Tool set for tightness, cleaning tools as cloth.... etc

  • Lifting device, press-on and detaching device.

  • Oil for bearing.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. تنظيف المبادل الحراري

  2. Examine visually bearing oil for cleaning, viscosity and color.

  3. كل 6 أشهر: carry out oil analysis and change it if required.
