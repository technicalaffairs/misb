Safety Precautions

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, breathing mask safety glasses etc.

  • Isolate the motor: electrical and mechanical.

Tools and Equipment

  • A complete tool set,

  • Grease, lubricating oil, Plastic brush.

  • Megger, Voltmeter

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Clean the motor by dry compressed air.

  2. Clean the insulation.

  3. Clean winding from dust using plastic brush then with a suitable wetted cloth.

  4. Check junction box terminals connections and insulation.



     

  5. Measure the insulation resistance as the following table:

Rated Volt Insulation Resistance DC Voltage Test
600V and blow 1.5 MΩ 500
2300 V 3.5 MΩ 5000
4000 V 5 M Ω 5000

If values are low, dry the winding by heating.

  1. Check the winding resistance.

  2. Change the ball bearing.
