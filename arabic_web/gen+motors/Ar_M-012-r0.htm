مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed every 3000 hours of operation. This procedure was applied in ABU QIR power plant since 1985 and the final draft is issued in May 2005

The first draft is initiated and tested by eng. Ahmed Maharem, accepted by eng. Khalil Aly Khalil general director of Abu Kir PS, approved by eng. Aly Anwar El-Fayoumy Head Sector of  Abu Kir PS, and certified by eng. Mona Ahmad El Sharkawy: WDEPC Inspection and Quality Control head sector.

Safety Precautions

  • A work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Current transformer isolation and proper earthing is required

Tools and Equipment

  • Megger, rags, brushes, Compressor for cleaning.

  • Alvania grease

  • Injection test set

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Clean the outer surface of the motor

  2. Clean the cooling paths using the compressed air or brushes

  3. Grease the motor (front and rear) bearing with alvania grease R3 , R2

  4. Check the earth cable if it is in good fixation

  5. Check the connection of the motor if it is in good fixation state

  6. Measure the temperature of the stator

  7. Check the protection devices

  8. Measure the winding isolation
