Safety Precautions

  • A work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Isolation and proper earthing is required

  • Before starting any work on the motor, check that it is switched off and blocked up for switching on again.

  • The unit should be out of service.

Tools and Equipment

  • Tool set for tightness, cleaning tools as cloth.... etc.

  • Crane, press ON, detaching device, properly fitting spanners,

  • Oil for bearing.

Work to be Carried Out

  1. Examine visually the bearing oil: oil level and oil viscosity.

  2. Every six months carry out oil analysis and if required change the oil.

  3. General cleaning.
