مقدمة

This document outlines the maintenance procedures P2, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block

The first draft initiated by eng. Ahmed Fawzy Badry, tested by eng. Nabeel saad Ibrahem at 01 July 2003, accepted by general director eng. Shawqy Mahmoud Attala, and approved by eng. Mohamed Mahdey Rezq Alnshar Head Sector of Sidi Kirir 1&2 Power Station.

السلامة الاحتياطات

  • يجب إصدار تصريح العمل والصيانة المؤهلة يجب أن يحمل الموظفون فئة السلامة المناسبة،

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

    •

  • Wear safety helmet, and safety shoes.

  • The breaker of the motor must be isolated

  • The motor windings and capacitors must be discharged used a solid wire first before touching the windings

  • The motor is stopped and electrically isolated before check

أدوات والمعدات

  • Electrical equipment kit, megger 5000V.

  • Dry cloth, dry air from air blower or surface line

  • New filters if necessary

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Carryout the MPIS document No. M-002-r0 every two months

  2. Clean the inside of the motor using dry air

  3. Open the junction box and check the cable terminals tightening, tighten any loose bolt if any

  4. Clean the junction box and capacitors surfaces.

  5. Measure the winding insulation using megger 5000v
