مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, which must performed on weekly bases as indicated by W1 in the title block.

يستخدم هذا المحرك للمعدات التالية: 

Boiler feed water pump, desalination water supply pump, fire fighting motor, closed cooling motor, service water pump, circulating water pump, condensate extraction pump

المسودة الأولى بدأها المهندس. Mohamed Elbiaa, tested by eng. Haytham Abd El Mohsen, reviewed by eng. Mohamed Abbass Ahmad, revised by EDEPC quality control eng. Adel Abd el-Kader El Naggar, and approved by eng. Tarek Youssef Ali  Head Sector of Ayoun Moussa Power Station.

تم قبول هذه الوثيقة من قبل المهندس. Samir Abdel Gilel: EDEPC inspection and quality control general director & MPIS coordinator, and certified by accountant: Mahmoud Mohamed Soliman EDEPC admin. & financial inspection, quality control general director and supervisor of inspection & quality control's head sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the qualified maintenance staff must carry the suitable ID safety category.

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear the appropriate safety and healthy equipment as safety shoes, helmet ... etc

  • Motor is out of service for some works if necessary

  • High temperature may arise under operating conditions on the motor surfaces so that touching should be prevented or avoided.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Kit, أمان and healthy equipment

العمل المراد تنفيذه

 

  1. تحقق من ذلك لا يوجد أي ضجيج أو اهتزاز غير طبيعي

  2. Check the oil level for upper bearing

  3. Check the air filter is cleaned
