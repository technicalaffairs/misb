مقدمة

Boiler Hot Gases Pass And Dumper used for transfer exhaust gases from G.T.

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued

  • Wear safety helmets, shoes

  • HRSG خارج الخدمة.

  • Put damper selector on close position.

  • Disconnect the power supply of damper actuator and tag it.

  • Gas turbine out of service.

  • ويجب اتباع كافة احتياطات السلامة قسم السلامة

Tools and Equipment

  • Grinder & grinding wheel.

  • Complete maintenance tools set.

  •  welding machine.

  • Manual puller.

Works to be Carried Out

  1. Check H.G. Pass Stainless steel Sheets for Tightness.

  2. Check H.G. Pass for good Insulation for duct.

  3. Check Damper Insulation & Stainless steel sheets .

  4. Check Damper Movement (Open-Close)

  5. Check Damper Open-Close Signals

  6. Check Damper wires and grease it if needed .

  7. Check damper gear box & its oil Re-fill if need .

  8. Check all parts by visual inspection .
