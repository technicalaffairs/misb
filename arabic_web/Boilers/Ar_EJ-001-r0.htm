مقدمة

This document outlines the inspection steps I1 for the metal expansion joints, (zigzag

under the evaporator (HP & LP) & blows at the frame of the boiler and at GT stack, Material: 15MO3), which must be carried out when  the gas turbine have major maintenance after 4000 work hour.

The first draft initiated by eng. Abdo Abdo El  Hendawy, tested by eng. Ashraf Abdel Moniem Fahmy at 28 April 2002 on Boiler no. 4 and revised by EDEPC quality control eng. Samir Abdel Gilel, accepted by general director eng. Sobhy Mohamed El Bakry and approved by eng. Moneer Mohamed El Wakeel Head Sector of Demiatta PS.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category,

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area,

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes.

  • HRSG out of service

  • افصل الطاقة توريد مشغلات المثبط ووضع علامة عليها.

  • G T out of service and (excitation CB & unit aux. CB & low voltage trans. CB) is rack out

  • ماك السقالات في مشترك إذا لزم الأمر

  • All safety precaution must be followed by safety department.

    •

Tools and Equipment

  • Argon and TIG welding machine    

  • Grinder m/c & grinding discs

  • حشو المعدن للأرجون واللحام TIG.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Modify the zigzag under HP evaporator to bellows

  2. إصلاح التشوهات

  3. لحام الشقوق بعد الطحن
