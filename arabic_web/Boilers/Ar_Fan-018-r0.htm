مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1 which must be performed on monthly bases as indicated by M1 in the title block.

The function of the F.D.F is to provide the combustion air to meet the boiler demand under varying conditions quickly and efficiently.   

Technical Specifications of Fan:

Power = 2900 KW

Psuc. = 1 atm = 104mm H2O

RPM = 1000

Pdisc. = 1095 mm H2O
Tc = Ambient Q = 853200 Kg/h/Fan
Medium = Air Two fan per unit, system B.A

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear safety helmet, shoes, gloves etc.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of the fan motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of fan motor

  • The fan must be isolated mechanically by closing delivery damper

Tools and Equipment

  • Complete maintenance tools set

  • Cleaning fluid manual puller with a puller fabricated in the workshop

  • Dial indicator

  • Vibrometer straight edge

 

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check static parts for deteriorated paint and for scaling

  2. Inspect impeller for deposits and wear

  3. Check damper and vane control assemblies for grease and protect unpainted surfaces with rust ban or sprayed fuel oil

  4. يفحص blader for freedom of movement

  5. Check fan flexible connections and joint for damage

  6. Check coupling clearance 6-10mm

  7. Check inboard and outboard bearing clearance
