مقدمة

This document outlines the major maintenance procedure P1, which must be carried out when

the gas turbine have major maintenance after 40,0000 workhour.

The technical Data:
- Stober double damper type ( E ) electrical actuator drive,
- There are 8 actuators ( 4 isolate & 4 control ).

 المسودة الأولى بدأها المهندس. الكحلاوي مصطفى موسى اختبار م. عبد القادر فتح الله 02 April 2003 on Boiler no. 4 and revised by EDEPC quality control eng. Samir Abdel Gilel, accepted by general director eng. Sobhy Mohamed El Bakry and approved by eng. Moneer Mohamed El Wakeel Head Sector of Demietta PS.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category,

  • Safety fence (rope) with caution marks surrounding the work area,

  • Wear your safety helmet, shoes

  • HRSG is out of service.

  • Disconnect the power supply of damper actuators and tagged it.

  • Isolat the air tanks and depressing it.

  • G .T. out of service (excitation CB & unit auxiliary CB &low voltage trans. CB) is rack out.

  • ماك السقالات أسفل المخمدات الالتفافية.

  • كل السلامة يجب اتباع الاحتياطات من قبل شخص قسم السلامة.

Tools and Equipment

  • الهواء المطرقة وأداة التثبيت                                                                                      

  • Kit of key and aln

  • Seal cord, center bearing & flanged bearing & rivet & bolts & packing

  • Welding filler & welding machine

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. تحقق من تآكل العمود في المحمل المركزي {اللحام والطحن} إذا لزم الأمر.

  2. Check flanged bearing {replacement the defect and greasing).

  3. التحقق من استبدال محمل المركز العيب.

  4. Replace the bad packing for stuffing box and tight it.

  5. قم بتغيير سلك الختم السيئ للشفرات.

  6. Weld the cracks of the frame and repair the deformation parts.

  7. Change the mechanical drive defects for actuators.

  8. Repair or change the actuators gear box if defect.

  9. Check the air (lines & valves & tanks & hoses & pistons) and repair or change if necessary.

  10. فحص جميع (البراغي والصواميل) بحثاً عن شفرات المخمدات وتغيير الخلل.

  11. فحص قضيب الربط وإصلاح الخلل أو تغييره.

  12. Adjust the damper blades at( close and open )states mechanically arranging with instrument department.
