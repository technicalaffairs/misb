مقدمة

This document outlines the details equipment inspection steps I1, which must be performed on a monthly bases indicated by M1 in the title block.

This pump is used for supply the boiler with mazout.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the qualified maintenance staff should cany the suitable safety category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmets shoes, gloves, etc

  • Isolate the circuit breaker for the motor of tills pump

  • Isolate the electrical cable feeding of the motor of this pump

  • يجب عزل المضخة ميكانيكياً عن طريق إغلاق صمام الشفط والتوصيل

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة أدوات الصيانة الكاملة.

  • Cleaning fluid, manual puller.

  • Suitable lifting device feeler gauge.

  • Hammer, torque wrench, Dial indicator.
