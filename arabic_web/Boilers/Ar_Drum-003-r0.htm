مقدمة

هذا توضح الوثيقة إجراءات الصيانة المدرجة في P1 والتي يجب أن تكون يتم إجراؤها على أسطوانة البخار كل 3 سنوات كما هو موضح بواسطة Y3 في العنوان أعلاه كتلة.

The steam drum separates the water from the steam and returns the water by way of the down comers.

The first draft initiated by eng. Adel Atia  tested by eng. Abd El-Azim Abdin,  and revised by EDEPC quality control engineer Samir Abd El-Gelil , accepted by general director eng. Yosry Abd Elmaqsod, approved by eng. Awad Fathy  head sector of Talkha Power Station, and certified by eng. Aly Mostafa Al Lethy: EDEPCo. of Inspection and Quality Control Head Sector.

Safety Precautions

  • A work permit must be issued and the qualified staff should be carry the suitable safety category .

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • Wear your safety helmet, shoes, .. etc.

  • The HRSG are out of service and depressurized the high pressure section.

  • Drain the high pressure drum and put red tag on the feed water pumps.

  • مخمدات المدخل هي مغلقة، مقفلة ميكانيكية.

  • Disconnect the power supply for the damper actuators.

 

Tools and Equipment

  • Complete maintenance tool set.

  • Cleaning fluid, Rages.

  • طاحونة وعجلة الطحن.

 

Work to be carried out

  1. Remove front and rear manholes doors insulation.

  2. فتح باب فتحة التفتيش الأمامية.

  3. قم بإزالة الحشية القديمة.

  4. تنظيف الأجزاء الداخلية.

  5. Inspect the following parts and repare if needed.

  6. Drum doors for pits, cracks and damage.

  7. Drum internals for pits, cracks and damage.

  8. Drum body for pits, cracks and damage.

  9. Check water level glass and change if needed.

  10. Reinstall new gaskets.

  11. Close drum manholes doors.

  12. أعد التثبيت عزل أبواب غرف التفتيش.
