مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection checklist I1, which must be performed every month as indicated by M1 in the title block.

The two condensate transfer P/Ps (2 per unit) move the collected condensate in condensate tank to condensate return header to reboiler feed tank the Mazout day tank suction heater drain to the day tank area condensate tank from which two condensate transfer P/Ps discharge to reboiler خزان التغذية

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب إصدار أمر العمل و يجب أن يكون موظفو الصيانة المؤهلون فئة السلامة المناسبة

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required.

  • ارتداء خوذة السلامة والأحذية والقفازات إلخ.

  • Isolate the circuit breaker of pump motor

  • Disconnect electric terminals of motor

  • The pump must be isolated by closing delivery valves

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Standard complete tool set.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check mechanical seal for leakage, change the mechanical seal if needed.

  2. تحقق من اهتزاز ضجيج غير عادي وإصلاح سبب الاهتزاز عن طريق فحص المحاذاة بين المحرك والمضخة.

  3. Check for excessive vibration.

  4. Check pump discharge press received valves.

  5. Check for abnormal noise and repair the internal parts of the pump or check the motor.

  6. Tighten all loose bolts.

  7. Inspect the propeller and  impeller and ground or clean and then make the balance if needed.

  8. Change pump gasket.
