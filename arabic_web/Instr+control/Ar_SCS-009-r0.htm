مقدمة

This document outlines the inspection check list I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block.

The first draft initiated by eng. Sawsan El_Lethy, tested by eng. محمد السحراوي at 27 Aug. 2003, reversed by EDEPCo. quality control  eng. Abdel Menem AL Emam, and approved by eng. Mohamed Goda EL_Said General Director of Port Said G.T. Power Station, certified by eng. Aly Mostafa Al-Lethy, EDEPCo. Inspection and Quality Control Head Sector.

احتياطات السلامة

  • يجب أن يتم إصدار تصريح العمل يجب أن يكون موظفو الصيانة مؤهلين ويحملون فئة السلامة المناسبة

  • سياج أمان مع علامات تحذير مطلوب المحيطة بمنطقة العمل.

  • يرتدي the personal protective equipment as (safety shoes, Helmet).

  • Turbine shout down & all MCC CB off (racked out).

الأدوات والمعدات

  • جهاز قياس AVO الرقمي، جهاز قياس التردد، بطاقة المزيل.

  • مجموعة معايرة السرعة ترونيك الموديل  (151A4925)

  • Special tool to adjust the potentiometer at the cards

Magnetic Pickup ( 77HC1 , 77HC2 )

  1. يفحص DC resistance shod be (140Ω).

  2. Check wires for grounding.

  3. تأكد من أن المستشعر الأسلاك صحية.

إذا !vmlcom.endif Speed Control Card SSZB  (1L 1I ) 

  1. Inject frequency source from calibrator at pin (1C, 2B) at 100%, to reach NHP (5105) RPM.           

  2. Ensure that voltage at 5H,G read 10 V dc

  3. If it is not correct adjust from (R112).

  4. Pull card SFPB at 1L 1P.

  5. Make Jumper max1, max2 pin (34 & 35) at card SSKC to max. (VCE).

  6. Adjust 100% NHP until speed control light just comes on. If it is not correct adjust from (R112) according the table blow

Full Speed No Load  (FSNL)

  1. Remove card SFPB.     

  2. Set 100% NHP 5105 r.p.m.

  3. Measure VCE 7H,1G at calibrator as given in sitting (3.4 volt) if it is not correct. Must be adjusted from FSNL R207 at SFPB card.

Droop 104%

  1. Set 100% (NHP)  by 1C , 2B at calibrator

  2. Raise D.S.P to 104 at 4 volt

  3. Ensure that VCE =16.3 or 8 volt.

  4. If it is not correct. it must be adjusted from DROOP R209 at card SFPB.
