مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed every six months as indicated by M6 in the title block.

The technical specification of gear box, power rating 1800 kw input speed 90.58 RPM, output speed 1000 RPM, gear ratio 0.9058, oil quantity 550 L

The first draft is initiated, tested and approved by Eng. Abd El Mohsen Mohammed general manager of 

Nag Hamady hyd. PS

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work order must be issued and the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category,

  • سياج أمان مع مطلوب علامات التحذير المحيطة بمنطقة العمل.

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ...etc.

  • Stop the crane and switch off the power supply

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Set of wrenches, Screw driver

  • Grease for lubricaying

  • Cleaning material

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check up control and safety instrument

  2. Check up the noise of electrical pump

  3. Check up the level of oil in gear box tank

  4. Check up the bearing temperature of gear box

  5. Check up the noise at operation
