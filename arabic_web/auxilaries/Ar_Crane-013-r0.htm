مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I3, which must be performed every five years as indicated by Y5 in the title block   This document for the cranes at turbines hall of units # 1,2 & 3,4

The first draft initiated by eng. Eid Lotfy, tested by eng Ibrahem Youssef, revised  by quality control  eng Adel Abd- El kader El Nagar at EDEPC, accepted by eng Sayed Farag: general director, and approved by eng. Mokhtar Abd El-Latif Mady: head sector of Ataka PS

هذا الوثيقة مقبولة من قبل المهندس. سمير عبد الجليل EDEPC التفتيش ومراقبة الجودة المدير العام، منسق MPIS، و معتمد من قبل المحاسب محمود سليمان EDEPC المشرف والتفتيش المالي مدير عام مراقبة الجودة ومشرف التفتيش والجودة السيطرة على قطاع الرأس.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and the maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • مطلوب سياج أمان مع علامات تحذيرية تحيط بمنطقة العمل

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes, safety belt ...etc.

  • Shut down the power supply of the crane.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Tool set for tightness, Grease

  • Cleaning tools as cloth.... etc

  • Press-on and detaching device, air blower.

  • AVO meter, megger..etc

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Operate the crane, observe and hear the sound of main hoisting, auxiliary hoisting, trolley, and crane travel motors

  2. Grease motors bearing.

  3. General cleaning for all motor, and electronic control panel, resistors, by air blower.

  4. Check the motors breakers for main hoisting, auxiliary hoisting, trolley and crane travel and change the motors breakers if necessary.

  5. Check the main power supply line and change if necessary.

  6. Check the contactors, auxiliary contactors, timers and change if necessary.

  7. Operate the crane for test.

  8. Place a copy of the field test results at the equipment data file.
