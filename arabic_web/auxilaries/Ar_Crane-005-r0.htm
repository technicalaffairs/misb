مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I2, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 as indicated in the title block.

The technical specification of trash rack cleaning machine main dia. 4120*3250*3840 mm, traveling speed 2.2/22m/s, lifting speed 7m/s

ال تم بدء المسودة الأولى واختبارها والموافقة عليها بواسطة المهندس. عبد المحسن محمد المدير العام ل 

Nag Hamady hyd. PS

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintanance staff should carry the suitable safty category

  • Safty fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, أمان shoes.

  • Stop pump and switch off the power supply

  • أغلق صمامات المخرج والدخول

  • Put suitable warning boards

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة من الشدات، معدات المحاذاة

  • Grease for lubricating, Cleaning material

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check up the crane during operation and review the lifting mechanism for trash rack cleaning machine

  2. Check up the mechanical parts, rail, vibration test test the brake for the crane

  3. Check up the oil level in reduction gear box, control and safety instrument

  4. Check up the structure for the crane. Review the joints, rivits, welding and bolts

  5. فحص نظيفة و دهن الحبال والبكرات والوصلات

  6. Operate the crane for test
