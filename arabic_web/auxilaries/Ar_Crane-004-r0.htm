مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on a monthly bases as indicated  by M1 in the title block

The technical specification of trash rack cleaning machine main dia. 4120*3250*3840 mm, traveling speed 2.2/22m/s, lifting speed 7m/s

The first draft is initiated, tested and approved by eng. Abd El Mohsen Mohammed general manager of Nag Hamady hyd. PS        

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued and qualified maintanance staff should carry the suitable safty category

  • Safty fence with caution marks surrounding the work area is required

  • Wear the safety and healthy equipment as safety helmet, safety shoes ...etc.

  • Stop pump and switch off the power supply

  • أغلق صمامات المخرج والدخول

  • Put suitable warning boards

الأدوات والمعدات

  • مجموعة من الشدات، معدات المحاذاة

  • Grease for lubricating, Cleaning material

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check up the crane during operation and review the lifting mechanism for trash rack cleaning machine

  2. Check up the mechanical parts, for carriage and test the brake

  3. Check up the oil level in reduction gear box, control and safty instrument

  4. Check up and clean the mechanical parts for the crane, lifting gear motor

  5. Check up the greasing mechanism for the carriage and lifting for drum

  6. Operate the crane for test
