مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, which must be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

The technical specification of platform crane is lifting capacity 300 ton - lifting speed 2.2 m/min

The first draft initiated by eng. Wagdy Roshdy Habeb, tested by eng.Samy Shehata Ebrahim, accepted by eng.  Hanafy Fahmy Madny general director, approved by eng. Abd El Kerim Abu Zeid Ismail Head Sector of High Dam Hydraulic Power Station, and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem: HPGC technical inspection general director, and MPIS Coordinator.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding work area is required

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as helmet, shoes .... etc

  • Switch off the AC and DC

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Set of wrench, special screw drivers

  • Cleaning material

  • Compressed air supply

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Test crane operation along its stroke up and down from EL. 118 to EL. 160 and check up the operation of the mechanical parts

  2. Check up operation of the stoppers in EL 160 - 143 - 126

  3. Check up the structure, repair any defects, clean and paint

  4. Check up the alignment of the electric motors with gearbox and synchronizing shaft and the drum

  5. Check up the bolts and coppling

  6. Check up the breaks, shoes and clearance, Check up the break pulley

  7. Check up the all bolts for rope:
    - Check up the bearing and motion transmision gears and change the grease
    - Clean the rope

  8. Check up the synchronizing shafts, covers, bearings and change the grease

  9. Check up the gearboxes and check up its oil level:
         - Check up gear and bearing
         - Check up the bolts

  10. Check up the fixed pulleys and guides and change the grease

  11. Check up and gears the mechanical parts for lifting crane

  12. Check up the oil lines under the platform

  13. Operate the crane up and down and operate the stoppers

  14. Operate the platform with load
