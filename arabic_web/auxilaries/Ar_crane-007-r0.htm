مقدمة

This document outlines the equipment inspection I1, to be performed on an annually bases as indicated by Y1 in the title block

The technical specification of crane No. (1) Semi - Gantry crane with lifting capacity 400 - 32 - 3.2 ton

The first draft initiated by eng. Wagdy Roshdy Habeb, tested by eng. Samy Shehata Ebrahim, accepted by eng.  Hanafy Fahmy Madny general director, approved by eng. Abd El Kerim Abu Zeid Ismail Head Sector of High Dam Hydraulic Power Station, and certified by eng. Kamel Ragheb Hakem: HPGC quality control general director, and MPIS Coordinator.

احتياطات السلامة

  • A work permit must be issued, the qualified maintenance staff should carry the suitable safety category.

  • Safety fence with caution marks surrounding work area is required.

  • Wear safety and healthy equipment as helmet, shoes .... etc.

  • Stop the crane and switch off the power supply.

الأدوات والمعدات

  • Safety helmet, safety shoes ... etc.

  • Set of wrench, screw drivers.

  • Grease for lubricating.

  • Cleaning material.

العمل المراد تنفيذه

  1. Check up the crane during operation and review the lifting mechanism for cranes 400 - 32 - 3.2 ton.

  2. Check up the mechanical parts for the carriage.

  3. Check up the oil level in reduction gear boxes.

  4. Check up and test the brake.

  5. Check up the vibration on the crane and on the carriage.

  6. Check up control and safety instrument.

  7. Check up the rail for the crane and for the carriage.

  8. Check up the structure for the crane and for the carriage. Review the joints, rivits, welding and bolts.

  9. Check up and clean the mechanical parts for the crane, carriage and reduction gear boxes.

  10. فحص الميكانيكية أجزاء الفرامل والوصلات.

  11. Check up and clean the lifting mechanism for the main crane.

  12. Check up, clean and grease the ropes, check up the pulleys and the hook.

  13. Check up and clean the carriage cable, check up the bolts and the grease.

  14. Check up the greasing mechanism for the carriage - the grease on the mechanical parts.

  15. Operate the crane for test.
